## How Sniping Works

1. **Domain expires** — owner doesn't renew
2. **Grace period** (30-45 days) — owner can still renew at normal price
3. **Redemption period** (30 days) — owner can renew at premium (~£100-300)
4. **Pending delete** (5 days) — queued for release
5. **Drop** — domain becomes available. Drop-catch services grab it in milliseconds.
