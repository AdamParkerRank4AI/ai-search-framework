# PART 1: HOW TO GET CITED IN AI OVERVIEWS

## Page Structure That AI Pulls From

Every page on your comparison sites should follow this format:

```
H1: Best Invoice Finance Companies UK 2026
   [2-3 sentence direct answer — AI pulls this]

[COMPARISON TABLE — structured data AI can parse]
| Company | Rate | Min Facility | Speed | Rating |
|---------|------|-------------|-------|--------|
| ...     | ...  | ...         | ...   | ...    |

H2: How Much Does Invoice Finance Cost?
   [Direct answer with specific numbers in first sentence]
   [Then detailed breakdown with worked examples]

H2: How Does Invoice Finance Work?
   [Step-by-step numbered list — AI loves these]

H2: Invoice Finance vs Factoring — What's the Difference?
   [Direct comparison — captures "X vs Y" AI queries]

H2: Frequently Asked Questions
   [Each Q&A is a potential AI Overview answer]
   Q: What is the cheapest invoice finance provider?
   A: [Direct answer with name and rate — first sentence]

[Schema markup: FAQPage, Article, Product]
```

## The 7 Rules for AI Citation

### 1. Lead with the answer
AI pulls the first 1-2 sentences after a heading. Put the answer FIRST, then elaborate.

**Bad:** "Invoice finance is a complex topic with many factors to consider. In this guide we'll explore the various options available to UK businesses..."

**Good:** "The best invoice finance companies in the UK for 2026 are Close Brothers, Aldermore, and Bibby Financial Services, based on our analysis of rates, speed, and minimum facility sizes."

### 2. Use specific numbers and data
AI cites pages with statistics. Make them up nowhere — use real data.

- "Average invoice finance fees range from 0.5% to 3% of turnover"
- "Processing time: 24-48 hours for most providers"
- "Minimum facility sizes start from £10,000"

### 3. Build comparison tables
Tables are the most AI-parseable content format. Every comparison page needs one.

### 4. Add FAQ schema markup
FAQPage schema tells Google "these are direct Q&A pairs." AI Overviews pull from FAQ schema heavily.

### 5. Create interactive tools AI can't replicate
Calculators, eligibility checkers, quote request forms. These force clicks because AI can't provide them.

- "Invoice finance cost calculator"
- "Heat pump grant eligibility checker"
- "Solar savings estimator"
- "Compare quotes from 3+ providers"

### 6. Demonstrate real experience (E-E-A-T)
- Named author with credentials ("Written by [Name], 15 years in invoice finance")
- Author page with photo, bio, LinkedIn link
- Original research, proprietary data, genuine reviews
- "We contacted 47 providers and compared quotes" beats "here are the top 10 based on our research"

### 7. Keep content fresh
AI favours recency. Update comparison tables quarterly. Add "Last updated: April 2026" prominently. Pages not updated quarterly are **3x more likely to lose citations.**

### 8. Write "answer capsules" (THE #1 tactic)
After every H2/H3 heading, write a **40-60 word self-contained factual block** that directly answers the heading. This is the single most common trait among AI-cited content. Over 90% of cited capsules contain no links — they're clean, extractable fact blocks.

### 9. Include original data and statistics
Adding statistics improves AI visibility by **41%** (Princeton/Georgia Tech study). Pages with original data tables earn **4.1x more AI citations.**

Create branded stats: "Our analysis of 47 invoice finance providers found that 63% charge between 0.5-2% of invoice value." This creates a citation hook AI can't find elsewhere.

### 10. Use definitive language — don't hedge
AI prefers sources that commit to positions. Cited content is 2x more likely to use "is defined as" or "refers to" rather than "may", "could", or "it depends."

### 11. Build 5+ pages per topic (topical authority)
**86% of AI citations come from sites with 5+ interconnected pages on the topic.** A single comparison page won't get cited. You need a cluster:
- Pillar: "Best Invoice Finance Companies 2026"
- Cluster: "Invoice Finance Costs Explained"
- Cluster: "Factoring vs Invoice Discounting"
- Cluster: "How to Choose an Invoice Finance Provider"
- Cluster: "Invoice Finance for Recruitment Agencies"
- Cluster: "Invoice Finance FAQ"
- Content clusters drive ~30% more organic traffic and hold rankings 2.5x longer

### 12. Build brand mentions across the web
Brand web mentions have the **strongest correlation (0.664) with AI visibility** — stronger than backlinks or traditional rankings. Get mentioned on:
- Industry press, guest posts, expert quotes
- Reddit (genuine participation in relevant subreddits)
- Wikipedia (if relevant articles exist for your topic)
- YouTube (23% of AI Overview citations)

### 13. Don't block AI crawlers
Ensure robots.txt allows: GPTBot, PerplexityBot, CCBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended. If you block them, you can't get cited.

### 14. Optimise for Bing (not just Google)
ChatGPT and Perplexity index primarily from **Bing**, not Google. Submit your sitemap to Bing Webmaster Tools. Many SEOs ignore this — it's a massive competitive advantage for AI visibility.

# PART 2: MULTI-CHANNEL DISTRIBUTION (The Automation Plan)

## Why Multi-Channel Matters

AI search engines pull from multiple surfaces:
- **Google** → your website
- **YouTube** → video content (23% of AI Overview citations!)
- **Reddit** → discussions mentioning your brand (21% of citations)
- **LinkedIn** → B2B authority content
- **Instagram/TikTok** → brand awareness → brand mentions → AI training data

Every piece of content should exist on ALL these surfaces. One blog post becomes 5+ pieces of content.

## Content Formats AI Cites Most

| Format | Citation Share | Why |
|--------|---------------|-----|
| **Listicles** (ranked lists) | 21.9% | #1 format — structurally aligned with how AI generates recommendations |
| **Articles** (long-form) | 16.7% | Authority content with depth |
| **Product/service pages** | 13.7% | Especially for "best X" queries |
| **Tables** | 2.5x multiplier | vs unstructured text |
| **FAQ content** | 3.2x more likely | to appear when using FAQ schema |

**Word count has near-zero correlation with AI citations.** Structure, specificity, and recency predict citations far better than length.

## Schema Markup Priorities (Install These First)

| Schema | Impact | Apply To |
|--------|--------|----------|
| **FAQPage** | **350% citation increase** | All FAQ sections on every page |
| **Article** | Helps AI identify content type, author, dates | Every blog post/guide |
| **Product** | For individual service entries | Each provider in comparison |
| **AggregateRating** | Comparison ratings and scores | Rating tables |
| **Organization** | Establishes brand identity | Site-wide |
| **HowTo** | For methodology/guide content | Step-by-step guides |

Use **Rank Math** or **Yoast** WordPress plugins to add these without coding.

## How Each AI Engine Picks Sources

| AI Engine | Primary Source | What It Prioritises |
|-----------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Google AI Overviews** | Google search index | Must rank top 10 first. E-E-A-T. Topical authority. |
| **ChatGPT** | Bing index + training data | Brand mentions (0.664 correlation). Reviews. Industry recognition. |
| **Perplexity** | Curated index | Recency. Reddit/forums (6.6% of citations). Structural clarity. |
| **Claude** | Training data | Authority sources. Structured content. Factual density. |
| **Gemini** | Google index | Schema markup. Entity relationships. Freshness. |

**Key insight:** Optimise for Google AND Bing. Google feeds AI Overviews + Gemini. Bing feeds ChatGPT + Perplexity. Most SEOs only optimise for Google.

# PART 3: PLATFORM-SPECIFIC TACTICS

## YouTube (23% of AI Overview citations — HIGHEST)

YouTube is the second most-cited source in AI Overviews. This is the single most important channel after your website.

**What to create:**
- "Invoice Finance Explained — Complete Guide 2026" (long-form)
- "Best Invoice Finance Companies UK 2026 — Compared" (comparison video)
- "How Much Does Invoice Finance Cost?" (FAQ video)
- Short clips: "Invoice Finance in 60 Seconds", "Factoring vs Invoice Discounting"

**Why it matters:**
- YouTube videos appear in Google search results directly
- YouTube transcripts are crawled by Google and AI models
- YouTube is owned by Google — inherent ranking advantage
- Videos with timestamps can appear as featured snippets

**Automation:** Pictory converts your blog → video. Opus Clip creates shorts. Buffer schedules uploads.

## Reddit (21% of AI Overview citations)

Reddit is now the #2 most visible site in Google after Wikipedia. AI models heavily cite Reddit threads.

**Strategy:**
- Identify relevant subreddits: r/UKPersonalFinance, r/smallbusinessuk, r/UKInvesting
- Contribute genuinely helpful answers (NOT spam)
- Naturally mention your comparison site when relevant: "I used compareinvoicefinance.co.uk to compare rates"
- Build karma and reputation over time
- Do NOT automate Reddit — authenticity is key, manufactured engagement is detected

**Why it matters:**
- Reddit threads rank for comparison queries
- AI models cite Reddit discussions
- Brand mentions on Reddit feed into AI training data

## Instagram / TikTok (Brand Awareness → AI Training Data)

These don't directly drive SEO, but they build **brand mentions** which AI models pick up.

**Content format:**
- Carousel posts: "5 things to know before getting invoice finance" (slide format)
- Reels/TikToks: 30-60 second explainer clips from your YouTube videos
- Behind-the-scenes: "How we research and compare providers"

**Automation:** Opus Clip creates short clips. Later/Buffer schedules posts.

## LinkedIn (B2B Lead Gen)

For B2B verticals (invoice finance, business loans, business energy), LinkedIn is where your buyers are.

**Content format:**
- Repurpose blog insights as LinkedIn posts
- Share comparison data and statistics
- Engage with industry conversations
- Build personal brand of your named expert/author

## Email Newsletter (Highest Converting Channel)

Email has the highest conversion rate of any channel. Build a list from day one.

**How:**
- "Get our free invoice finance comparison guide" → email capture
- "Subscribe for weekly rate updates" → ongoing engagement
- Weekly digest of new comparisons, updated rates, market news
- **Automation:** Mailchimp/ConvertKit, triggered by new blog posts via Zapier

## Pinterest (Long-Tail SEO Boost)

Pinterest pins are indexed by Google. Infographic-style comparison tables can rank for image search and drive traffic months later.

# PART 4: WHAT TO AVOID

## Content That Gets Killed by AI Overviews

| Content Type | Why It Dies | What To Do Instead |
|-------------|------------|-------------------|
| "What is invoice finance?" | AI answers this in full | "Invoice finance cost calculator" (interactive) |
| Generic "best X" listicles | AI synthesises these | Comparison with ORIGINAL test data |
| Manufacturer-copied descriptions | Zero unique value | Original reviews with real experience |
| Thin comparison pages | Keyword-swapped templates | Deep-dive comparison with specific data per provider |
| AI-generated content dumps | Targeted by Dec 2025 update | AI-assisted but human-edited with original insights |

## SEO Tactics That Are Now Dead

1. **Keyword stuffing** — has been dead for years but still worth saying
2. **Link schemes** — Google's SpamBrain is specifically detecting purchased links
3. **Thin affiliate content** — 71% hit rate in Dec 2025 update
4. **Content length for length's sake** — Google now distinguishes depth from length
5. **Ignoring E-E-A-T** — anonymous comparison sites without named experts are being demoted
6. **Ignoring video/YouTube** — 23% of AI citations come from YouTube

## The Expired Domain 301 Specifically

The Aug 2025 Spam Update targeted "expired domain abuse." But it specifically targets:
- Buying expired domains and putting UNRELATED content on them
- Buying hundreds of random domains to stack authority

**Your approach is safe because:**
- Your redirect domains are from the SAME industry (invoice finance → invoice finance comparison)
- You're doing 5-10 per vertical, not hundreds
- The destination sites have real, helpful content
- 301 redirecting an acquired domain is standard business practice
