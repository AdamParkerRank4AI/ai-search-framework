# The 7 Formats: One Ranking → One Week

Every compareSEO ranking table expands into these seven formats. Specs, frames, copy slots and hook templates below.

All dimensions assume:
- **Reels / stories:** 1080 × 1920 (9:16), safe zone 160 top / 280 bottom
- **Feed carousels:** 1080 × 1350 (4:5), safe zone 80 all sides

---

## Format 1 — Cover reel (Monday)

**Purpose:** Awareness. This is the reel we push hardest for reach. Its only job is to make someone stop scrolling and save.

**Length:** 6–9 seconds.

**Structure:**
| Frame | Seconds | What's on screen | Voiceover / caption |
|-------|---------|------------------|---------------------|
| 1 | 0.0 – 1.5 | Big number "10" taking up 70% of screen. Category title across the top. | "The top ten AI SEO agencies in the UK right now…" |
| 2 | 1.5 – 4.0 | Fast montage: positions 10 → 4 flash for ~0.3s each with the agency name and score. | "…ranked by AI visibility, citation rate and signal stability…" |
| 3 | 4.0 – 6.0 | Positions 3, 2 blurred with a lock icon. Text: "Full list in carousel ↓" | "…swipe to see who made the top three." |
| 4 | 6.0 – 9.0 | Static hold on the category title, compareSEO badge, QR code to rank4ai.co.uk. | (music only) |

**Text overlay template:**
```
TOP 10
[CATEGORY]
UK · 2026
Ranked by compareSEO
```

**Hook library (pick one per month, split-test):**
- "The top 10 AI SEO agencies in the UK — the full ranking."
- "We ranked every AI SEO agency in the UK. #1 surprised us."
- "If you hired an SEO agency this year, check this list."
- "Google just stopped mattering. These ten agencies get it."
- "Most SEO agencies can't even define AEO. These ten can."

**CTA:** "Full ranking in next post" + link in bio.

## Format 2 — 10-slide carousel (Tuesday)

**Purpose:** Saves + profile visits. Carousels own the "second chance at reach" slot: people save them, Instagram sees saves, Instagram pushes them again a week later.

**Slides:** 10 entries + cover + methodology + CTA = 13 slides max (IG cap is 20 but 10–13 converts best).

**Slide frames (1080 × 1350 each):**

### Slide 1 — Cover
```
TOP 10
AI SEO AGENCIES
UK · 2026
[compareSEO badge]
← swipe for the full ranking
```

### Slides 2–11 — one per agency (same layout, inverted for #1)
```
┌───────────────────────────────┐
│ #07                           │
│                               │
│ [Agency name, large display]  │
│                               │
│ Score: 74 / 100               │
│ Top signal: Subject Authority │
│ Weak spot:  Ecosystem         │
│                               │
│ "One-sentence verdict."       │
│                               │
│ compareseo.co.uk              │
└───────────────────────────────┘
```

**Visual rule:** position number is the single biggest element on the slide. Score is second biggest. Agency name is third. Reverses the hierarchy of a web table where the agency name would dominate.

### Slide 12 — Methodology

Full methodology → rank4ai.co.uk/methodology
```

```

→ link in bio

## Format 3 — Countdown reel (Wednesday)

**Purpose:** Reach via re-watches. Countdowns are one of the highest-retention reel formats on Instagram because people wait for #1.

**Length:** 25–30 seconds.

**Structure:** 10 cards, 2.5s each, hard cut between.

**Per-card template:**
```
#10                           ← huge, top
──────────────────────────
[Agency name]
74 / 100
"Strong on content depth,
 weak on entity clarity."
──────────────────────────
compareSEO · UK · 2026        ← small, bottom
```

Use a simple tick or whoosh sound between cards. Do **not** add background music with lyrics — kills comprehension on sound-off autoplay.

**On-screen captions (always enabled):** every card mirrors the text on screen. 85% of reels are watched muted.

**Hook (first 1.5s):** "Counting down the top 10 AI SEO agencies in the UK."

**CTA overlay (last 3s):** "Full methodology → compareseo.co.uk"

## Format 4 — Criteria carousel (Thursday)

**Purpose:** Authority. This is the post that turns "nice ranking" into "trusted ranking." It's also the evergreen post that gets linked in sales DMs forever.

**Slides:** 7.

| # | Slide | Copy |
|---|-------|------|
| 1 | Cover | "How compareSEO actually ranks AI SEO agencies" |
| 2 | The 5 signals | Identity · Authority · Architecture · Ecosystem · Consistency |
| 3 | Signal 1 detail | What we measure, how it's scored, example |
| 4 | Signal 2 detail | " |
| 5 | Signal 3 detail | " |
| 6 | Signal 4 detail | " |
| 7 | Signal 5 detail + CTA | "This is the same framework we use in the Rank4AI audit" |

**Why this post matters:** it converts scrollers into believers. The ranking is only trusted if the methodology is visible. Put this carousel in a highlight as "Methodology" and link it from every other post.

## Format 5 — #1 spotlight reel (Friday)

**Purpose:** Controversy + shares. One reel dedicated entirely to the top entry — why they won, what they do differently, and (tactfully) what #2 did wrong.

**Length:** 20–30 seconds, talking-head + screen recording split.

**Structure:**
| Seconds | Visual | Voiceover |
|---------|--------|-----------|
| 0 – 3 | Face-to-camera, bold text "Why [Agency] is #1" | Hook: "We ranked 47 UK AI SEO agencies. One of them scored 19 points higher than everyone else. Here's why." |
| 3 – 15 | Screen recording of the agency's site + an AI query showing the agency cited | "They do three things nobody else does: [1], [2], [3]." |
| 15 – 25 | Face-to-camera, split screen vs #2 | "The runner-up is strong on [X] but loses to them on [Y] — the single biggest ranking factor." |
| 25 – 30 | Outro card with compareSEO badge + CTA | "Full ranking and scores → link in bio." |

**Legal note:** always frame as "our ranking, our methodology, our opinion." Never assert private data about a competitor. Stick to publicly observable AI output.

## Format 6 — Movers carousel (Saturday)

**Purpose:** Turns a static ranking into recurring content. Every monthly refresh produces a new movers post — free content forever.

**Slides:** 6.

```
Slide 1 — Cover          "↑↓ Who moved this month"
Slide 2 — Biggest riser  "+4 positions — [Agency] — why"
Slide 3 — Biggest drop   "−3 positions — [Agency] — why"
Slide 4 — New entry      "First time on the list — [Agency]"
Slide 5 — Dropped out    "Fell out of top 10 — [Agency]"
Slide 6 — CTA            "Live ranking → compareseo.co.uk"
```

**Hook (caption):** "Three agencies moved this month. One fell out of the top 10 completely. Here's what happened."

This post is the **recurring engine** of the account. Once the ranking refreshes monthly, this format guarantees one guaranteed-good post per month with near-zero effort.

## Format 7 — Stories quiz (Sunday)

**Purpose:** DMs + link clicks. Stories are where follows convert into conversations.

**Sequence:** 5 story frames.

1. **Question sticker:** "Guess who's #1 on this month's ranking?" — harvest replies, each reply opens a DM thread.
2. **Poll sticker:** "Are you using an AI SEO agency? Yes / Not yet"
3. **Quiz sticker:** 4-option multiple choice — "Which of these is in the top 3?" with one correct answer.
4. **Countdown sticker:** "Full ranking drops every 1st of the month."
5. **Link sticker:** direct to rank4ai.co.uk/audit with "Your free AI visibility score →".

**DM follow-up template (for every reply to story 1):**
> "Good guess — [correct answer] actually topped the list this month. Want me to send you the full ranking + the methodology we used? It's a free PDF, takes 30 seconds."

The quiz story is the single highest-converting asset on the entire account because it **starts a conversation** with a warm, opted-in viewer.

---

## Summary: what to build for every new ranking

1. 1 × cover reel (9s)
2. 1 × 13-slide carousel
3. 1 × countdown reel (30s)
4. 1 × 7-slide methodology carousel (reuse month-to-month)
5. 1 × #1 spotlight reel (25s)
6. 1 × 6-slide movers carousel
7. 1 × 5-frame stories quiz

Total working time once templates exist: ~3 hours per ranking.
