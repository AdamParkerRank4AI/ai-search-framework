# Monthly refresh playbook

Every compareSEO ranking updates on the 1st of the month. This document is the checklist for turning that refresh into one full week of Instagram content — every month, forever, with a consistent format.

The whole point of the design system is that this cycle becomes mechanical. No brainstorming. Just plug the new data into templates.

---

## T-7 to T-1 (the week before the 1st)

- [ ] Pull the previous month's published posts from the scheduler — what were the top 2 best-performing formats? Note them for doubling down.
- [ ] Pull the next month's ranking CSV as soon as it's locked internally.
- [ ] Run a `diff` between this month and last month — that diff is the raw material for the Movers carousel (Format 6).
- [ ] Export all agency logos / screenshots you'll need into `/compareSEO-IG/YYYY-MM/[ranking-slug]/assets/`.
- [ ] Block 2 hours on Adam's calendar for face-to-camera recording (Format 5 spotlight).

---

## T-0 (1st of the month, publish day for Cover reel)

Post Format 1 — **Cover reel** — at the peak slot for the account (currently best guess: Tue 7pm UK, will A/B over first 60 days).

- [ ] Upload via scheduler, don't post live from phone (IG gives marginally better reach on scheduled professional posts in 2026)
- [ ] Auto-comment the first comment with `"Full ranking in the next post — drops tomorrow. Save this."`
- [ ] Pin the auto-comment
- [ ] Manually respond to first 20 comments within 60 min of publishing — this window is where the algorithm decides to keep pushing or not

---

## T+1 — Carousel drop (Format 2)

The 13-slide carousel is the "proof" post and the one sales DMs link back to. Treat it as the hero asset of the week.

- [ ] Publish 9am UK (carousels do better mid-morning than evening — people save them for later)
- [ ] First comment: `"Slide 12 is the methodology — pinned for anyone who asks 'how did you rank these?'"`
- [ ] Save to the "Top 10 Rankings" highlight immediately
- [ ] Cross-post to LinkedIn as a native carousel (different file, same content) — free distribution

---

## T+2 — Countdown reel (Format 3)

- [ ] Publish 6pm UK (reels perform best 5–8pm)
- [ ] Overlay "Saved for later?" text in final frame — explicit save prompt lifts save rate ~20%
- [ ] Cross-post to TikTok and Reels-on-Facebook via Meta Business Suite
- [ ] First comment: `"Which one surprised you most? Reply with the position."`

---

## T+3 — Criteria carousel (Format 4)

The only evergreen post of the week. This one can be reused month after month with tiny tweaks. On month 1, build it once. On every subsequent month, just update the date badge.

- [ ] Publish 9am UK
- [ ] Pin to profile after first run (only unpin for a better-performing post)
- [ ] Link from every future post's first comment: `"Our methodology is in this carousel: [link]"`

---

## T+4 — #1 spotlight reel (Format 5)

The biggest risk/reward post. Controversy-adjacent because we're singling out a runner-up.

- [ ] Legal check: every claim is about *publicly observable AI output*, not private performance data
- [ ] Publish 7pm UK
- [ ] Pin auto-comment linking the full ranking
- [ ] Monitor for 24h — this post is where SEO-community pile-ons happen, reply politely and with data
- [ ] If the runner-up's team objects, offer them an updated audit. Never escalate.

---

## T+5 — Movers carousel (Format 6)

The recurring engine. This post is the reason monthly refreshes compound.

- [ ] Publish 10am UK (Saturday low-competition window)
- [ ] First comment: `"Next update drops 1st of [NEXT_MONTH]. Follow for the reshuffle."`
- [ ] Add to "Movers" highlight

---

## T+6 — Stories quiz (Format 7)

- [ ] Publish 9am UK
- [ ] Set up DM auto-reply for Question sticker replies (template in `caption-templates.md`)
- [ ] Check DMs at 2-hour intervals during the day — this is the warmest traffic of the week
- [ ] Every reply is a warm lead for a free audit

---

## T+7 — Post-mortem

- [ ] Pull reach, saves, shares, profile visits for each of the 7 posts
- [ ] Score each post on three metrics: reach, saves, audits-booked-traceable
- [ ] Update the "what's working" doc with the winning format for this month
- [ ] Double-down plan for next month: the top-performing format gets 2 posts instead of 1, the bottom gets dropped or reformatted

---

## Month-on-month growth rules

- **Keep formats, swap hooks.** The 7 formats don't change for the first 6 months. Hooks and captions do.
- **Don't add formats to fill gaps.** If a format dies, replace it with a new one tested on stories first.
- **Only scale what works.** If cover reels are 3x the reach of countdowns, start posting two cover reels per week instead of one countdown.
- **Refresh the methodology post quarterly.** Keep it alive by re-pinning with an updated date stamp.
- **Never rebuild the design system.** 12 months minimum before touching the colours, type or grid. Consistency compounds; rebranding resets.

---

## Success milestones

| Milestone | Month | Signal |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| First 10k reel | 1–2 | Format + audience fit is proven. Lock the winning format. |
| First 50k reel | 2–3 | Content is travelling outside the account. Double posting rate. |
| First 100 audits booked in a month | 3–4 | Funnel works end to end. |
| 10k followers | 4–6 | Account is compounding; cut the 2-reel-a-day cadence to 1. |
| First press pickup of a compareSEO ranking | 4–8 | Rankings are now newsworthy. Add a press pack to the refresh cycle. |
