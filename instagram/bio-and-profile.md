## What NOT to do on day 1

- Don't add a Linktree. One direct link to compareseo.co.uk. Linktrees kill click-through.
- Don't use a personal photo as the profile pic. The brand IS the product — the logo builds recognition faster than a face on a media account.
- Don't put "DM for collabs" in the bio. That's for influencer accounts. compareSEO is a publisher.
- Don't add emojis to the bio. The dark, clean, text-first aesthetic starts in the bio.
- Don't set it as a Creator account. Business accounts get better analytics and the ability to schedule via third-party tools.
