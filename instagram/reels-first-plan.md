# Reels-first execution plan (no design tools required)

The founder-friendly, zero-Canva, zero-Figma version of the compareSEO Instagram strategy. This file is the **primary execution plan** if Adam is running the account himself with just a phone.

The 7-format carousel-heavy system in `post-formats.md` is the "with-a-designer" version. Keep it for reference — but until there's a VA or retainer designer, the plan below is what we actually run.

---

## The principle

Carousels need design software. Reels don't.

Reels need: a phone, a mouth, and CapCut (free). Every "ranking" post format in the original plan has a reel equivalent that can be made in under 20 minutes on a phone without touching Canva or Figma.

Founder-led talking-head reels also outperform polished brand carousels for accounts under 10k followers in 2026. So this isn't a compromise — it's arguably the better strategy for the first 6 months.

---

## The 7-reel week (replaces the 7-format carousel week)

Every time compareSEO publishes or updates a ranking, we ship this in the same week. Every item below is made on a phone in <20 minutes.

| Day | Format | How | Tools | Time |
|-----|--------|-----|-------|------|
| Mon | **Cover reel** — "We ranked every UK AI SEO agency. The #1 surprised us." | Phone front camera + text overlay in CapCut | CapCut free | 10 min |
| Tue | **Countdown reel** — #10 → #1, 3s each, text + tick sound | CapCut: 10 identical cards chained | CapCut free | 15 min |
| Wed | **Talking-head hot take** — "Three things that won them #1" | Phone front camera, Instagram native reel editor | IG app | 5 min |
| Thu | **Methodology reel** — "How we actually rank them (the 5 signals)" | Phone front camera, text overlay | IG app or CapCut | 10 min |
| Fri | **#1 Spotlight reel** — Adam talking head + ChatGPT screen recording | Phone + Loom + CapCut | CapCut + Loom | 20 min |
| Sat | **Movers reel** — "Three agencies moved this month. Here's why." | Phone front camera | IG app | 15 min |
| Sun | **Stories quiz** — native Instagram stickers | Instagram app only | IG app | 5 min |

**Total production time per week: ~1.5 hours.** All on a phone. Zero design software.

## How to make each reel without Canva

### 1. Cover reel (Mon)

**On screen text only — no face-to-camera needed.**

1. Open CapCut → new project → pick a 9:16 blank canvas
2. Solid black background clip (CapCut has free "solid colour" clips)
3. Add text: "TOP 10 / AI SEO AGENCIES / UK · 2026" — pick a bold default font, yellow colour
4. Add the agency names as flash cards for positions 10 → 4 (0.3s each, hard cuts)
5. Add a "full list in next post" outro
6. Export
7. Background music: CapCut trending audio tab

**No design software. The entire "design" is the text tool inside CapCut.**

### 2. Countdown reel (Tue)

The production pattern to memorise:

1. Make **one** 3-second card in CapCut: solid black, big yellow "#NN", agency name, score, verdict
2. **Duplicate that card 10 times** (CapCut has a right-click duplicate option)
3. Change the position number, name, score and verdict on each copy
4. Chain them together, add a tick sound on each cut
5. Add a hook text overlay on the first frame: "Counting down the UK's top 10 AI SEO agencies"
6. Export

**This is the killer of Canva Bulk Create in reel form.** You build one 3-second card, duplicate ten times, swap text. Takes 15 minutes once you've done it once.

### 3. Talking-head hot take (Wed)

1. Prop phone against something, front camera, 4K 30fps
2. Film 30 seconds of Adam talking: "Three things that put [Agency] at #1 on our UK ranking this month"
3. Open the Instagram app → new reel → upload video
4. Instagram's native reel editor has auto-captions (tap the speech-bubble icon) — turn them on
5. Add one text overlay for the hook in the first 1.5 seconds
6. Post

**Zero editing, zero external tools.**

### 4. Methodology reel (Thu)

Same as #3 but different script: "How we rank them — the 5 signals."

Adam lists them in 60 seconds to camera. Instagram auto-captions. Done.

### 5. #1 Spotlight reel (Fri)

The only reel that needs CapCut because it combines two sources:

1. Record a 15s Adam intro on phone: "We ranked every UK AI SEO agency. One scored 19 points higher than everyone else. Here's why."
2. Record a 10s Loom screen capture of asking ChatGPT "best AI SEO agency UK" and showing the answer
3. Open CapCut → stitch them together → add a one-line text overlay naming the agency
4. Export

**Tools: your phone for footage, Loom for the screen recording, CapCut to chain them. No Canva.**

### 6. Movers reel (Sat)

Same as #3: phone + Instagram native editor. Adam says which three agencies moved and why. 30–60 seconds.

### 7. Stories quiz (Sun)

Open Instagram app → Story → use the built-in **Question**, **Poll**, **Quiz**, **Countdown** and **Link** stickers. Five frames, five minutes, no design work at all. Instagram's native stickers *are* the design.

## What you need to buy

| Tool | Cost | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| **CapCut** | Free | Desktop and mobile. The only video editor you need. |
| **Loom** | Free tier is enough | Screen recordings of AI queries (for the Friday spotlight reel) |
| **Later** or **Metricool** | £10–20/mo | Schedule reels in advance so you're not posting live at 7pm every day |

**Total monthly spend to run the whole strategy: ~£15/mo.**

No Canva Pro. No Figma. No Adobe. No agency retainer.

---

## The one rule: batch-record

Do all of Adam's talking-head footage in **one 60-minute session per week**. Monday morning, coffee, phone on a tripod, filming 7–10 clips back-to-back. That single session produces every talking-head reel for the week.

The failure mode is trying to film "whenever I feel like it" — you'll film nothing and the account dies. Block the calendar, do it in one go, ship the whole week in a batch.

---

## When to add carousels back in

Once one of these things is true, layer carousels back into the rotation:

1. You've hired a VA (£15/mo from Upwork or OnlineJobs.ph) who handles the monthly Canva Bulk Create refresh — see `production-pipeline.md`
2. The account passes 5k followers and one of your reels hits 50k views — at that point a retainer designer starts paying for itself
3. You personally sit down for a 2-hour Canva tutorial on YouTube and build the master yourself

Until one of those happens, the 7-reel week above is the plan. Ship it.

---

## The fallback ladder

If you get stuck on any step, move down the ladder:

1. Try to film the reel on your phone → stuck?
2. Record on your phone, hand the raw file to a VA with a 1-line brief → stuck?
3. Skip that format for the week. Post 6 reels instead of 7. The account won't die.

**Shipping 6 average reels beats shipping 0 perfect carousels.** The whole playbook only works if content actually goes out.
