# Caption Templates

Fill-in-the-blanks captions for every format. Square brackets are variables. Every caption ends with the same three-line signature block for brand consistency.

---

## Global rules

1. **First line is the hook.** Instagram truncates captions after ~125 characters — the first line has to earn the "more" tap.
2. **Never bury the ranking category.** Always say "AI SEO Agencies UK" or whatever the list is — the keyword does work in search.
3. **Always end with a CTA.** Link in bio, swipe, save, DM. Pick one per post.
4. **Hashtags go at the bottom, 10 max.** See the three-tier hashtag set.
5. **Use the signature block verbatim** on every post:

```
──
compareSEO · independent rankings by Rank4AI
compareseo.co.uk
```
