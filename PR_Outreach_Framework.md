# Rank4AI: PR Outreach Framework
## Building Entity Presence at Scale

**Author:** Adam Parker, Founder, Rank4AI

**Purpose:** A structured, cost-effective approach to generating high-volume third-party mentions across publications, podcasts, directories and media platforms to strengthen Signal 04 (Ecosystem Validation) across all AI platforms.

---

## Core Principle

AI platforms verify entities through independent, third-party co-occurrence. Volume of consistent mentions across credible sources matters more than a single high-profile placement. The goal is saturation of the entity graph, not vanity press.

Every mention must reinforce the same identity signals:
- **Entity name:** Rank4AI
- **Founder:** Adam Parker
- **Category:** AI Search Strategy / AI Visibility
- **Positioning:** The framework for getting businesses recommended by AI platforms
- **Differentiator:** Signal-based methodology (Five Signal Model), not traditional SEO

---

## Tier Structure

### Tier 1: Free — Journalist and Expert Platforms

**Cost:** £0
**Effort:** 30-60 minutes per day
**Expected volume:** 5-15 placements per month

These platforms connect journalists with expert sources. AI search is a hot topic. Responding consistently generates quoted mentions in published articles.

| Platform | Type | Notes |
|----------|------|-------|
| Qwoted | Journalist requests | Free tier available. Respond to marketing, SEO, AI and tech queries |
| SourceBottle | Journalist requests | Australian-founded, global reach. Good for marketing and business press |
| Help a B2B Writer | B2B focused | Strong for SaaS, marketing and technology publications |
| Featured.com | Expert quotes | Connects sources with writers at major publications |
| Terkel | Expert roundups | Lower authority but high volume. Every mention counts |
| PressPlugs | UK journalist requests | UK-focused. Good for regional and trade press |
| ResponseSource | UK journalist requests | Established UK platform used by national and trade journalists |

**Process:**
1. Set up profiles on all platforms using identical identity language.
2. Set alerts for: AI, search, SEO, digital marketing, AI strategy, ChatGPT, visibility.
3. Respond to every relevant request within 2 hours (speed wins placements).
4. Always include full name, company name and one-line descriptor in every response.
5. Track every placement in the Outreach Tracker (see below).

### Tier 2: Free — Podcast Circuit

**Cost:** £0
**Effort:** 2-3 hours per week (including recording time)
**Expected volume:** 8-12 appearances per month

Podcasts generate multiple signals per appearance: a published episode page, show notes with backlinks, social media promotion by the host, and often a written summary or transcript. One appearance can create 3-5 indexed mentions.

| Platform | Type | Notes |
|----------|------|-------|
| PodMatch | Guest matching | AI matches you with relevant shows. Free tier available |
| Podchaser | Podcast discovery | Search for marketing, SEO and AI shows. Pitch directly |
| MatchMaker.fm | Guest matching | Good for business and marketing podcasts |
| Podcast Guests | Guest directory | List yourself as available. Hosts come to you |
| LinkedIn direct outreach | Manual | Search for podcast hosts in marketing/AI/SEO space and pitch directly |

**Pitch template:**
> I run Rank4AI, a framework for helping businesses get recommended by AI platforms like ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It's a fundamentally different approach to traditional SEO — based on signal integrity rather than keywords. I can talk about why most businesses are invisible to AI search, what the AI platforms actually look for, and what businesses can do about it now before the window closes. Happy to tailor to your audience.

**Target podcast categories:**
- Digital marketing and SEO
- AI and technology
- Small business and entrepreneurship
- SaaS and B2B marketing
- Founder stories and startups

## Reactive PR: Newsjacking

**Cost:** £0
**Effort:** 15-30 minutes per day
**Expected volume:** 2-5 mentions per month (variable)

When AI search news breaks (Google AI Overviews changes, ChatGPT updates, new AI platforms launching), be the first expert to comment publicly.

**Process:**
1. Monitor Google Alerts for: "AI search", "AI overviews", "ChatGPT search", "Perplexity", "AI recommendations", "AI SEO".
2. When news breaks, post a take on LinkedIn and X within 1 hour.
3. Email 5-10 journalists who cover AI/search with a ready quote and brief expert commentary.
4. Pitch a rapid-response article to 2-3 publications.

Journalists on deadline need sources fast. Being available and responsive builds relationships that generate repeat coverage.

## Outreach Tracker

Maintain a simple spreadsheet to track all outreach activity and placements.

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Date | Date of outreach or placement |
| Tier | Which tier (1-6) |
| Target | Publication, podcast or platform name |
| Type | Guest article, quote, mention, podcast, directory |
| Status | Pitched, accepted, published, rejected |
| URL | Link to published placement |
| Entity signals | Does it include brand name, founder name, link, category |
| Notes | Any follow-up actions |

Review weekly. Report monthly. Adjust effort allocation based on which tiers produce the highest volume of published placements.

## Quarterly Review

Every quarter, assess:

1. **Volume:** Total placements across all tiers. Target: 200+ per quarter.
2. **Entity accuracy:** Are placements using correct identity language?
3. **AI platform impact:** Test AI platforms with queries like "What is Rank4AI?" and "Who helps businesses with AI search?" to measure inclusion rate changes.
4. **Tier performance:** Which tiers are delivering the best volume-to-effort ratio? Shift budget accordingly.
5. **Freelancer performance:** Is the outreach freelancer hitting targets? Replace if below 15 placements per month.
6. **New opportunities:** Identify new publications, podcasts or platforms to add to the target list.
