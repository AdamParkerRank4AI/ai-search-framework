## Network-brand overhang

AI systems (per TendorAI research on AI mortgage-broker recommendations across 10 UK cities) disproportionately recommend **London & Country, Habito and John Charcol** even when a searcher specifies a city. The reason is directory density and press citations, not review volume. Any independent Colchester firm that wants to be cited for a Colchester query needs a denser local signal graph than those nationals — concretely: a better Companies House record, a visible FCA number, a richer schema stack, and consistent directory NAP.

## Where the displacement wedge is

Reading across the incumbents, three gaps stand out:

1. **Quantified evidence on the new entity.** We Are Mortgages wins on numbers. To compete the new site needs its own numbers on day one — total lending since 2014, total clients, median completion time, retention rate. These are Signal 02 evidence, not marketing copy.
2. **Merger transparency as an AI-legible story.** No Colchester competitor is in a fresh merger event. A well-structured public record of "RBFA → MMB Ltd from 1 April 2026" is unique, citable and the sort of temporal anchor AI systems pick up to stabilise the entity (Signal 05). Executed well it is a compounding asset; executed poorly it reads as graph drift.
3. **Topic depth on post-2024 UK mortgage reality.** Incumbents' content tends to be evergreen 101-level articles. Depth opportunities include:
   - Bank of England rate cycles and how fixed-vs-tracker choices change under them.
   - Post-2024 affordability rule changes (FCA MCOB updates, stress-test changes).
   - BTL in Colchester around the University of Essex rental market.
   - Military housing around Colchester Garrison (forces help-to-buy, CAAS, Forces Help to Buy 2 scheme).
   - Remortgaging ex-Help-to-Buy equity loans as they hit their 5-year interest point.
   - Self-employed / contractor affordability with 2025–2026 lender criteria.
   None of these are currently owned by a Colchester-local firm in any depth.

## Intent-mode map (Rank4AI Signal 02 / "Full Prompt Spectrum")

To meet Signal 02 the new site must answer queries across all four intent modes:

| Intent mode | Representative queries | Asset required |
|-------------|------------------------|----------------|
| Exploratory | *"how do UK mortgages work in 2026"*, *"is now a good time to remortgage"*, *"what is a buy-to-let mortgage"* | Long-form guides, glossary. |
| Diagnostic | *"why was my mortgage application declined"*, *"mortgage affordability self-employed 2026"*, *"how do I remortgage if my fix ends and I'm in negative equity"* | Problem-pattern pages, each with named adviser byline. |
| Transactional | *"mortgage broker near me"*, *"first time buyer broker Colchester"*, *"btl mortgage broker CO4"* | Service-area landing pages; GBP; booking CTA. |
| Navigational | *"my mortgage broker ltd"*, *"rishi bansropun mortgage"*, *"oliver gwinnell my mortgage broker"*, *"rb financial advisers colchester"* | Branded home, director bios, merger notice; 301s from the RB Financial estate. |

The navigational row matters disproportionately during the merger window: anyone searching for the old brand must land on a page that *explains the merger in entity terms* AI can parse, not a generic homepage.
