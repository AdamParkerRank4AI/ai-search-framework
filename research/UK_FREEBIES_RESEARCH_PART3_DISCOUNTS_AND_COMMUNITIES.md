### 6. Coupon Stacking Strategy ("Three Pillars")

**Pillar 1:** Supermarket loyalty app (Clubcard/Nectar/Lidl Plus)
**Pillar 2:** Manufacturer cashback app (Shopmium/GreenJinn/CheckoutSmart)
**Pillar 3:** Card-linked offer (Amex Offers/Barclays/Monzo cashback)

**Example Boots Stack:**
- Clubcard/Loyalty Price reduction
- Boots own-brand 10% off (Advantage Card)
- Personalised app offer (e.g. 150 bonus points)
- Student discount 10% (if applicable)
- Pay with accumulated Advantage Card points
- Claim CheckoutSmart/Shopmium cashback on receipt

**Realistic savings:** GBP 6-12 per shop, GBP 300-600 per year. Individual items can go net-free.
