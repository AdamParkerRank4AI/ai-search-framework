# SECTION 7: LOW QUALITY OR TRAP SOURCES TO AVOID

## Red Flags: Signs an Opportunity Is a Trap

1. **Asks for excessive personal info** beyond name/email/address (phone, DOB, bank details, NI number)
2. **Requests payment details** for a "free" product (subscription trap)
3. **No actual product named** - form asks about household income, pet ownership (data harvesting)
4. **No privacy policy or promoter identity** visible
5. **Offer appears nowhere else** on reputable sites (MSE, LatestFreeStuff, HotUKDeals)
6. **Unrealistically high-value prizes** (hundreds of iPhones, GBP 500 vouchers) with no real conditions
7. **Social media page created recently** using well-known brand name
8. **No T&Cs or promoter contact** (required by UK CAP Code)
9. **Demands sharing with multiple friends** to "unlock" the prize
10. **Excessive urgency** ("Act now", "Expires in 10 minutes")
11. **Poor spelling/grammar**, excessive emojis and dollar signs
12. **"Free plus shipping" with GBP 8-15 shipping** for small items
13. **Promises GBP 1,500/month testing products** (scam)
14. **Requires upfront fee** to join (e.g. GBP 50 "registration")

## Specific Sources to Avoid or Use With Caution

| Source | Problem | Risk Level |
|--------|---------|------------|
| MyOffers / Testers Keepers | Permission marketing model. Your data IS the product. Heavy spam calls and emails | MEDIUM |
| Facebook Amazon review groups | Sellers offer refunds for 5-star reviews. Violates Amazon TOS. DMCC Act 2024 makes this UNLAWFUL | HIGH |
| "Free plus shipping" offers | Inflated shipping covers product cost + margin. Often subscription traps | HIGH |
| Juicer.deals and similar Amazon coupon sites | Seller-funded review schemes. Violate Amazon TOS. Account suspension risk | HIGH |
| Unsolicited emails offering free products for reviews | Likely scam. 90% increase in Amazon impersonation scams in 2025 | HIGH |
| Physical letters inside Amazon packages demanding 5-star reviews for PayPal payments | Scam. Report to Amazon | HIGH |
| Any site requiring card details for a "freebie" | Almost certainly a subscription trap | VERY HIGH |
| Survey sites promising GBP 50+/hour | Unrealistic. Best survey sites (Prolific) pay GBP 5-8/hour | HIGH |
| Bounty (baby freebies) | Historically sold data to insurance/photography companies for aggressive upsells | MEDIUM |

## Signs an Opportunity Is Genuinely Worth It

1. Listed on multiple reputable aggregator sites
2. Backed by a recognisable brand with a real website
3. Clear T&Cs with promoter name and address
4. No payment details required
5. Realistic prize value for the entry method
6. Community members on HotUKDeals/MSE confirm receipt
7. Available via established platforms (Gleam, SoPost, Shopmium)

# SECTION 8: RECOMMENDED FIRST MONITORING STACK FOR A BOT

## Architecture: changedetection.io + Telegram Bot + Browser Automation

### Tool: changedetection.io
- **What:** Free open-source website change monitor
- **Setup:** Self-host via Docker or pip install
- **Capabilities:** Monitor static + dynamic pages, 85+ notification channels (Discord, Telegram, email, webhooks)
- **Browser Steps:** Log into sites, accept cookies, navigate before checking
- **Conditional alerts:** Only trigger when specific keywords appear or prices drop below thresholds
- **Scale:** Basic VPS handles 50 monitors. 500+ URLs needs decent hardware

### Layer 1: Telegram Channel Monitoring (Fastest Alerts)
Monitor these Telegram channels via Telegram Bot API (Telethon/Pyrogram):

### Layer 3: Browser Automation (Playwright/Puppeteer)
For semi-automated competition entry:
1. Bot opens competition page
2. Bot prefills form fields (name, email, address, DOB)
3. Bot handles cookie popups and navigation
4. Human reviews and clicks submit (handles CAPTCHA)
5. Bot logs entry in spreadsheet

**Recommended stack:** Playwright (Microsoft) - cross-browser, built-in auto-wait that mimics human behaviour, native Python support

### Layer 4: Email Automation
