## Lead Gen Opportunities for Rank4AI

### Potential Differentiators vs Listed Agencies
1. **UK-based** - Most agencies listed are US-based. Strong opportunity in UK/European market.
2. **Five Signal Model** - Proprietary, structured framework (vs ad hoc approaches).
3. **Multi-platform coverage** - Framework explicitly covers ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Copilot.
4. **Signal Consistency focus** - Unique emphasis on temporal stability that most agencies overlook.
5. **Audit methodology** - Scored 17-section audit mapped to five signal layers.

### Prospect Categories
- **Businesses currently using traditional SEO agencies** who need AI search strategy
- **B2B SaaS companies** seeing declining organic traffic from AI search disruption
- **Professional services firms** (legal, financial, consulting) needing AI visibility
- **UK/European businesses** underserved by US-dominated agency landscape
