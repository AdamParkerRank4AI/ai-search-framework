## TOOLS FOR NEXT STEPS

1. **Check domain availability**: Use Nominet WHOIS (nominet.uk/lookup/) for .co.uk/.uk domains, or who.is for .com domains
2. **Check backlink profiles**: Use Ahrefs, Moz Link Explorer, or Majestic to assess DR/DA and backlink quality
3. **Check Wayback Machine**: archive.org to verify domains had real content and weren't parked
4. **Bulk check**: Use expireddomains.net to filter by keyword and TLD for recently deleted .co.uk domains
5. **PowerfulDomains.co.uk**: Curated expired .uk domains marketplace with SEO metrics

## ADDITIONAL SEARCH STRATEGIES

If none of the above yield available domains with backlinks, try:

1. **Companies House Advanced Search**: Search dissolved companies with SIC codes 64191 (financial intermediation), 64921 (factoring), 64929 (other credit granting), 66190 (financial auxiliary services)
2. **FCA Financial Services Register**: Search for cancelled authorisations in lending/credit categories
3. **London Gazette**: Search insolvency notices for finance/lending companies
4. **Bridging & Commercial magazine archives**: Lists of failed bridging/commercial finance companies
5. **Alternative Credit Investor / P2P Finance News archives**: Comprehensive coverage of failed P2P platforms
6. **Insolvency Service press releases**: gov.uk announcements about wound-up finance companies
