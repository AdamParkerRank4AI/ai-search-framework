## The New Search Landscape

The search world in 2026 has fractured into **five overlapping layers**:

| Layer | What It Means |
|-------|--------------|
| **SEO** | Ranking pages on Google (traditional) |
| **GEO** | Getting cited by generative AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) |
| **AEO** | Winning zero-click answers and featured snippets |
| **AIO** | Training AI with your brand data via structured content |
| **SXO** | Turning search into sales through UX alignment |

### Key Stats

- **31.3%** of the US population will use generative AI search in 2026
- Gartner predicts traditional search volume will drop **25%** by 2026
- Organic search traffic could decrease by **over 50%** as consumers embrace AI-powered search
- When an AI summary appears in Google, users click external links only **8%** of the time (vs 15% without)
- Google still sends **190x** more traffic to websites than ChatGPT
- ChatGPT processes approximately **2.5 billion** prompts daily
- About **65%** of ChatGPT usage qualifies as traditional search behaviour

## GEO - Generative Engine Optimization

GEO focuses on getting your content **cited** by AI engines rather than ranked in traditional SERPs.

### Core Principles

1. **Topic Authority Over Keywords**: Target broader subjects instead of specific keyword phrases. Generative engines don't operate on a ranking system like traditional search - they synthesise information from trusted sources.

2. **Citation-Worthy Content**: Original research, proprietary data, and expert commentary attract citations. If you publish something no one else has (a benchmark study, a unique dataset, a framework), AI engines have a reason to cite you over alternatives.

3. **Content Structure for AI Extraction**:
   - Concise paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
   - Bullet points and numbered lists
   - Clear H2/H3 headers phrased as questions
   - 40-60 word answers directly after question headers

4. **Brand Consistency Across Platforms**: Your brand description on LinkedIn should align with what appears on your site, Crunchbase, review platforms, and industry directories. AI systems use cross-platform consistency to categorise and reference brands with confidence.

5. **Cross-Functional Citation Strategy**: Combine structured on-site content, citation generation (PR coverage, content placement, Reddit/social signals), and model-friendly technical optimisations.

### GEO Trends for 2026

- Shift from keyword targeting to **topic targeting**
- Focus on **brand visibility and mentions** over ranking positions
- **40-60% of cited sources change month-to-month** across Google AI Mode and ChatGPT, making visibility far less stable than traditional organic rankings
- Only **7.2%** of domains get cited in both LLMs and Google's AI Overviews, indicating fragmented AI ecosystems

## AEO - Answer Engine Optimization

AEO focuses on structuring content so AI assistants and voice search can deliver your content as a **direct answer**.

### Key Techniques

- Structure content for **immediate answers** (question/answer format)
- Add **citations and statistics** to build authority
- Implement **structured data** to help AI engines understand context
- Include **FAQ sections** to answer questions directly
- Write **conversationally** - match how people actually ask questions

### AEO vs Traditional SEO

Instead of just returning links, search engines now deliver direct answers powered by LLMs. The content that wins isn't necessarily the most keyword-optimised - it's the most **clearly structured and authoritative**.

## LLMO - Large Language Model Optimization

LLMO aligns content to how LLMs **process, interpret, and prioritise** information, favouring semantic understanding, cohesion, and contextual relevance.

### 7 Ways LLMO Differs from SEO

1. **Semantic chunking** replaces keyword density
2. **Vector alignment** matters more than backlink profiles
3. **Retrieval optimization** over crawl optimization
4. **Machine-citable trust signals** over human trust signals
5. Focus on being **synthesised** rather than ranked
6. Content **freshness signals** work differently in LLMs
7. **Multi-platform presence** influences LLM training data

### What's Actually Working

- Structuring content with clear question/answer pairs
- Publishing original data and research
- Building presence on **high-citation platforms** (Reddit, LinkedIn, Wikipedia)
- Using **schema markup** extensively
- Creating content that's **quotable in 1-2 sentences**

| AI Platform | LinkedIn Citation Rate |
|-------------|----------------------|
| ChatGPT | 14.3% |
| Google AI Mode | 13.5% |
| Overall | 11% |

This puts LinkedIn ahead of Wikipedia, YouTube, and every major news site for AI citations.

### How to Optimise LinkedIn for AI Visibility

1. **Post frequently**: 75% of cited authors post at least **5 times per month**
2. **Original content only**: 95% of AI citations come from original posts
3. **Long-form articles**: LinkedIn articles are crawled by search engines and LLMs
4. **Complete your profile**: 100% profile completion with keyword-rich sections
5. **SEO your profile**: Add keywords to the beginning of your tagline and About section
6. **Avoid pure AI-generated content**: Authentic content avoids being flagged
7. **Add dates**: Include specific dates (e.g., "Top AI tools for 2026") to show freshness
8. **Use bullet points**: Easier for AI to scan and extract key insights

- Custom meta descriptions
- Custom slugs/URLs
- Keyword optimization within the platform

## Local SEO in the AI Era

### The Geolocation Problem

Generative AI **doesn't know where you're searching from** - at least not without being told explicitly. This lack of geolocation leads to generic, irrelevant answers. Traditional search still wins for queries with clear local intent.

### Local Ranking Factors (2026)

| Signal Group | Weight |
|-------------|--------|
| Google Business Profile | 32% |
| Reviews | 20% |
| On-page signals | 15% |
| Behavioural signals | 9% |
| Other factors | 24% |

### Key Local SEO Actions

1. **Google Business Profile**: Your highest-leverage asset for local visibility
   - Complete and accurate information
   - Real photos regularly (exterior, interior, team, work, before/after)
   - Seed Q&A sections with real questions and clear answers
   
2. **Multi-Platform Presence**:
   - Apple Business Connect (Maps, Siri, Wallet)
   - Bing Places for Business (feeds into Copilot)
   - Consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) across all platforms

3. **AI-Optimised Local Content**:
   - Question-and-answer-based structure
   - Location-specific pages with genuine local information
   - Local schema markup (LocalBusiness, GeoCoordinates)

4. **Review Strategy**: Reviews are the 2nd most important signal group at 20%

## Schema Markup & Structured Data

### Why Schema Matters More Than Ever

Structured data isn't just for traditional SEO - it makes your content **machine-readable and AI-friendly**. By adding schema markup, you help LLMs understand your content better.

### Priority Schema Types for AI

| Schema Type | Use Case |
|------------|----------|
| **FAQPage** | Q&A content - highly cited by AI |
| **HowTo** | Step-by-step guides |
| **Article** | Blog posts and news |
| **Product** | E-commerce listings |
| **Review** | Customer reviews and ratings |
| **Organization** | Brand information |
| **Author** | Expertise and authority signals |
| **LocalBusiness** | Local SEO |
| **Speakable** | Voice search optimization |

### Implementation Tips

- Focus on schema types that **answer questions directly**
- FAQPage and HowTo schema are most likely to be pulled into AI answers
- Use **Author schema** to establish expertise (important for E-E-A-T signals)
- Combine multiple schema types on a single page where relevant

## Weird & Wonderful Findings

Here's where it gets interesting. These are the surprising, counterintuitive, and sometimes bizarre findings from across LinkedIn and industry research:

### 1. Small Sites Can Beat Giants in AI Search
While Google consistently offers the same 10-15 domains, AI search engines cited **over 160 unique websites** including small players like Thrifty Traveler, Fang Wallet, and Finance Buzz. If you have quality, citable content, you can appear in AI answers regardless of your site's traffic or brand recognition.

### 2. Traditional Rankings Don't Predict AI Citations
**90% of ChatGPT citations come from pages outside Google's top 20**. A page ranking #50 in Google might be the #1 cited source in ChatGPT for the same topic. The correlation between traditional SEO ranking and AI citation is remarkably weak.

### 3. AI-Cited Traffic Converts 4.4x Better
Visitors arriving via AI citations convert at **4.4 times** the rate of traditional organic visitors. The theory: AI pre-qualifies the recommendation, so users arrive with higher intent and trust.

### 4. Reddit Dominates AI Citations at 40%
Reddit leads ALL sources at **40.1% citation frequency** across LLMs. This makes Reddit presence arguably more important than traditional link building for AI visibility.

### 5. The Click-Through Collapse
The CTR from ChatGPT is approximately **96% lower** than Google's. Users get their answers directly in the chat. The traffic game is shifting from volume to quality.

### 6. Monthly Citation Volatility
**40-60%** of cited sources change month-to-month. Unlike traditional SEO where rankings are relatively stable, AI visibility is highly volatile. You can be cited heavily one month and disappear the next.

### 7. LinkedIn Beats Wikipedia for AI Citations
LinkedIn appears in **11% of all AI responses**, outperforming Wikipedia for AI citations. LinkedIn articles and posts are treated as authoritative expert sources by AI engines.

### 8. Content Freshness Signals Are Critical
Adding specific dates (e.g., "The top AI tools for 2026") helps both audiences and AI systems recognise content as current. AI engines heavily favour content that appears up-to-date.

### 9. "As Seen In" Sections Work
Rewriting product descriptions to include scientific data, adding "As seen in" sections highlighting media mentions, and publishing expert articles with citations are all tactics that boost AI visibility.

### 10. The 5-Layer SEO Stack Is Now Required
Winning in 2026 requires mastering SEO + GEO + AEO + AIO + SXO simultaneously. Optimising for only one layer leaves significant visibility on the table.
