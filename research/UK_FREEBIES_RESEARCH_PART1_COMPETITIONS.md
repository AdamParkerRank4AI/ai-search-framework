## GLEAM.IO GIVEAWAY ECOSYSTEM

### How Gleam Works
- Widget-based entry embedded on brand websites or hosted on gleam.io
- Entry methods: form fill (name/email), social follows, shares, visits, referrals, custom actions, daily entry
- Supports JavaScript prefilling via `Gleam.push()` or URL parameters
- Fraud prevention: automated filter analysing 20+ attributes; CAPTCHA for suspicious users
- Country restriction: geo-blocking by country (MaxMind)

### UK Brands Using Gleam Repeatedly
- UK Planet Tools LTD (Makita, Bosch, Milwaukee, DeWalt)
- Lenovo (regular NA/EU/UK giveaways)
- Aosom (garden/home - Outsunny brand)
- NexiGo (tech/projectors)
- Savers Health and Beauty (Rimmel)
- Bristol Blue Glass (jewellery)
- My Baba (baby products)
- House of Coco (skincare)

- **2025 Rule Change:** Meta no longer allows requiring engagement (likes, comments, tags, follows) as mandatory entry. Can only be "encouraged"
- Discovery hashtags: #win, #competition, #giveaway, #UKGiveaway, #WinItWednesday

### MODERATELY AUTOMATABLE (4-6/10)

| Method | Score | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|

| Instagram follow+tag | 3/10 | Meta detects automation |
| Facebook competitions | 4/10 | Platform monitoring |
| Quiz/answer questions | 5/10 | Answers shared in comping communities |

### MANUAL ONLY (1-3/10)

| Method | Score | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Creative/effort comps | 1/10 | BUT: lowest entries = best odds |
| Phone/text entry | 2/10 | Manual phone interaction |
| Radio phone-in | 1/10 | Must listen live |
| Postal entry | 2/10 | Physical post required |

### Recommended Automation Tools
1. **RoboForm** - free browser extension, the "comper's favourite"
2. **Lightning Autofill** - Chrome extension, form filling + macros
3. **QuickForm** - Chrome extension with record mode and auto-click
4. **VeloFill** - AI form filler (Chrome, Firefox, Edge)
5. **Playwright/Puppeteer** - for semi-automated browser workflows (open page, prefill, human submits)

### Legal Position (UK)
- No specific UK law bans automated competition entries
- Competition T&Cs almost universally prohibit automated entries = disqualification
- Bypassing CAPTCHAs could engage Computer Misuse Act 1990
- **Recommended approach:** Semi-automation - bot opens pages and prefills, human reviews and submits

## REALISTIC WIN RATES

- Mass-entry win rate: approx 1 in 1,000
- Strategic/selective win rate: approx 1 in 60 (low-entry, creative, local)
- Professional comper (Di Coke): 25 entries/day, 150 wins/year, GBP 15,000 prize value
- All competition winnings are **TAX-FREE** in the UK
- Most experienced compers set aside 30-60 minutes per day

### Best Odds Strategy
1. Target creative/effort comps (fewest entries)
2. Target local competitions (very few entries)
3. Target short-lived Twitter comps (hours not weeks)
4. Enter daily entry comps consistently
5. Enter magazine dailies across all surviving titles
6. Use form-fill automation for web entries
7. Monitor Gleam aggregators for new UK giveaways
