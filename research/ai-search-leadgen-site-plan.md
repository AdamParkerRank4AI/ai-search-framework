## 301 REDIRECT DOMAINS — Stack Authority Into Primary

### Where to Find Cheap SEO/Marketing Expired Domains

**Step-by-step (do this yourself — needs browser access):**

1. Go to **ExpiredDomains.net** → Deleted Domains
2. Filter: keyword = "seo" OR "search" OR "marketing" OR "digital"
3. Filter: TLD = .com and .co.uk
4. Sort by: Backlinks (BL) or Domain Pop (DP) — highest first
5. Filter: Archive.org birth year before 2020 (want aged domains)
6. Cross-check top candidates on:
   - **web.archive.org** — does it have real expert content cached?
   - **ahrefs.com/backlink-checker** (free) — what's the DR and who links to it?
7. Register the best ones at £5-8 each on any registrar
8. Set up 301 redirects to your primary domain

### Specific Targets — Defunct SEO/Marketing Companies to Watch

Search Companies House (find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk) for recently dissolved companies with "SEO", "digital marketing", "search" in the name. Their domains often expire 6-12 months after dissolution.

**Search queries for Companies House:**
- "SEO" — filter by "Dissolved" status, date range 2024-2025
- "Digital Marketing" — same filter
- "Search Optimization" — same filter

Then check if their .co.uk domains are expiring or available.

### Types of 301 Domains to Look For

| Type | Example | Why |
|------|---------|-----|
| Defunct SEO agency | smallseoagency.co.uk | Has backlinks from client sites, directories |
| Dead marketing blog | seoexpertblog.com | Has content backlinks from industry sites |
| Failed SEO tool | sometool.io | Has tech press backlinks |
