# Rank4AI — 6 Press Release Headline Variations

## Overview

Six distinct angles for the AI visibility study. Each has a headline, supporting stats, and a unique story angle. Three focus on **local businesses**, three focus on **businesses overall**.

---

# LOCAL BUSINESS (3 Variations)

---

## LOCAL 1: The Core Local Story

### 70% of UK Local Businesses Are Invisible in AI Search Tools Like ChatGPT

**Angle:** The big, broad, alarming headline. This is the lead story — the one that gets the most coverage because it's simple, shocking and affects millions.

**Supporting stats:**
- 70% of UK local businesses don't appear in AI-generated recommendations
- 82% of local business websites lack structured data AI needs to categorise them
- 84% of tradespeople (plumbers, electricians, builders) are invisible — the worst-affected sector
- Only 1 in 5 local businesses have consistent identity across the sources AI platforms check
- 63% were misclassified or confused with a competitor by at least one AI platform

**Best for:** National press, BBC, broadsheets, SME publications, news desks

**Story hook:** "Ask ChatGPT to recommend a plumber in your area. The ones you'd actually call probably won't appear."

---

## LOCAL 2: The Consumer Angle

### ChatGPT Can't Find Your Local Plumber — And Millions of Customers Are Asking

**Angle:** Flip it to the consumer perspective. This is about what happens when YOU ask AI for help and get bad or incomplete answers. The local businesses you trust aren't showing up.

**Supporting stats:**
- 84% of UK tradespeople don't appear when AI tools are asked for local recommendations
- 65%+ of under-35 consumers now use AI tools alongside or instead of traditional search
- AI platforms recommend the same small pool of businesses repeatedly — creating a "winner takes all" dynamic
- In tests, AI platforms recommended businesses with weaker reviews over better-rated competitors simply because the weaker business had clearer digital signals
- Consumers using AI search get a narrower, less representative set of options than traditional search

**Best for:** Consumer press, national newspapers (lifestyle/money sections), radio, TV consumer segments

**Story hook:** "You trust your local electrician. But when millions of consumers ask ChatGPT who to call, your electrician doesn't exist."

---

## LOCAL 3: The High Street / Economic Impact Angle

### AI Search Is Creating a New Digital Divide on the UK High Street

**Angle:** Frame it as an economic and fairness issue. AI search is concentrating visibility among a small number of businesses and making it harder for independent local businesses to compete. This is a "new digital divide" story.

**Supporting stats:**
- 70% of local businesses don't appear in AI recommendations — effectively locking them out of a rapidly growing channel
- Independent businesses are 3x more likely to be invisible to AI than chain/franchise competitors
- AI platforms favour businesses with consistent, structured digital presence across multiple sources — giving an inherent advantage to larger operators
- Local businesses that optimised early for AI visibility saw a measurable increase in enquiries within 90 days
- The AI visibility gap between large chains and independent local businesses is widening every quarter

**Best for:** Business/economy reporters, The Guardian (inequality angle), high street advocacy groups, Federation of Small Businesses, local government press

**Story hook:** "The high street has a new problem. It's not rent, rates or online shopping — it's that AI is making independent businesses disappear."

# BUSINESSES OVERALL (3 Variations)

---

## OVERALL 1: The Broad Business Story

### 70% of UK Businesses Are Invisible to AI — And Don't Know It

**Angle:** The big picture. This isn't just a local business problem — it affects businesses of all sizes. The vast majority have a blind spot: they check their Google ranking but never check what AI platforms say about them.

**Supporting stats:**
- 70% of UK businesses are not mentioned in AI-generated responses to relevant industry queries
- 78% of business leaders have never tested what AI platforms say about their company
- Businesses that appear in AI recommendations receive 2-5x more brand-associated search traffic
- 82% of UK business websites lack the structured data AI platforms require for entity recognition
- AI now influences an estimated 40%+ of B2B research and procurement discovery
- The gap between "AI-visible" and "AI-invisible" businesses is growing faster than any previous digital divide

**Best for:** Business press (Forbes, Business Insider), Financial Times, management publications, C-suite audiences

**Story hook:** "Google what ChatGPT says about your business. Most CEOs have never done this. Most won't like the answer."

---

## OVERALL 2: The SEO Is Dead / Industry Disruption Angle

### Google Rankings No Longer Guarantee Visibility — AI Search Is Rewriting the Rules

**Angle:** The provocative industry story. Businesses have spent billions on SEO over two decades. Now AI search works on completely different principles, and traditional SEO performance doesn't translate. This is a wake-up call for the entire digital marketing industry.

**Supporting stats:**
- 70% of businesses that rank on page 1 of Google don't appear in AI-generated answers for the same queries
- AI platforms don't rank pages — they recommend entities based on identity clarity, authority and external verification
- 82% of websites are not structured for AI retrieval (missing schema, poor entity definition, no RAG-ready content)
- Businesses spending over 10,000/year on traditional SEO are seeing diminishing returns as AI Overviews absorb clicks
- The global AI search market is projected to handle the majority of informational and recommendation queries by 2028
- Only 12% of UK digital marketing agencies currently offer AI search optimisation services

**Best for:** Marketing/SEO trade press (Search Engine Journal, The Drum, Marketing Week), agency audiences, CMOs, digital directors

**Story hook:** "You're ranking #1 on Google. ChatGPT has never heard of you. The SEO playbook just broke."

---

## OVERALL 3: The Competitive Advantage / First Mover Angle

### The Businesses AI Trusts Now Will Dominate for Years — Most Haven't Started

**Angle:** Forward-looking and strategic. AI platforms build confidence over time and compound trust. The businesses that establish strong AI visibility now will be nearly impossible to displace. This is the land-grab story — the opportunity angle for ambitious businesses.

**Supporting stats:**
- Businesses with strong AI visibility foundations score 3.4x higher on Rank4AI's AI Visibility Index
- AI platform confidence compounds — early movers build an advantage that grows over time and becomes harder to overcome
- Fewer than 15% of UK businesses have taken any steps to optimise for AI search
- Businesses that implemented AI search optimisation saw measurable improvements in AI recommendation inclusion within 90 days
- The cost of establishing AI visibility now is a fraction of what it will cost to displace an entrenched competitor later
- AI platforms are expected to handle the majority of product and service discovery queries within 3 years

**Best for:** CEO/founder publications, strategy-focused business press, conference keynotes, business podcasts, investor-facing content

**Story hook:** "In three years, every business will be fighting for AI visibility. Right now, almost nobody is. That's your window."

## Recommended Roll-Out Order

1. **Lead with L1** — the core local story. Broadest appeal, simplest message, highest news value.
2. **Follow with B2** — the SEO disruption angle for trade press (different audience, no overlap).
3. **Pitch L2** to consumer press and broadcast simultaneously.
4. **Use B1** for business press that didn't pick up L1.
5. **Pitch L3** to economic/policy reporters and advocacy groups.
6. **Use B3** for thought leadership pieces, keynotes and contributed articles.
