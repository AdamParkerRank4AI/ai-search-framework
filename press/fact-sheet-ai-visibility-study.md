### Why Businesses Are Invisible

The top reasons local businesses fail to appear in AI recommendations:

1. **No structured data** — AI can't categorise what it can't read in a structured format
2. **Identity inconsistency** — Business name, description or category differs across website, Google Business Profile, Companies House, directories and social media
3. **No external verification** — Business only exists on its own website with no independent third-party confirmation
4. **Content not AI-readable** — Key information locked behind images, PDFs, login walls or poorly structured pages
5. **Entity confusion** — Business name is ambiguous or shared with other entities, causing AI misclassification

### What's Driving the Shift

- **ChatGPT** has over 200 million weekly active users globally (as of early 2025)
- **Google AI Overviews** now appear in the majority of UK search results
- **Perplexity** processes millions of queries daily with full citation transparency
- **Microsoft Copilot** is integrated into Windows, Edge and Office products used by millions
- Consumers increasingly ask AI for **recommendations**, not just information — this is a transactional shift
