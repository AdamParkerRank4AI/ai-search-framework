Best Business Loans

A curated list of leading business lenders. Compare loan sizes, term lengths, indicative rates, and typical time to funding.

Funding Circle

Peer-to-peer lender offering unsecured term loans for established SMEs.

  • Loan amount: £10,000 – £500,000
  • Term: 6 months – 6 years
  • Indicative APR: from 8.9%
  • Time to funding: 48 hours typical
  • Best for: Trading SMEs with 2+ years of accounts.

iwoca

Flexible short-term credit line with pay-as-you-draw interest, designed for small businesses.

  • Loan amount: £1,000 – £500,000
  • Term: Up to 24 months
  • Indicative rate: from 2% per month on funds drawn
  • Time to funding: Same day possible
  • Best for: Managing cash flow and seasonal demand.

Starling Bank Business Loans

Term loans and overdrafts for existing Starling business current account holders.

  • Loan amount: £25,001 – £250,000
  • Term: 1 – 7 years
  • Indicative APR: from 7%
  • Time to funding: Within days of approval
  • Best for: Existing Starling customers wanting integrated banking.

NatWest Business Loans

Secured and unsecured loans from a major UK high-street bank.

  • Loan amount: £1,000 – £10m+ (secured)
  • Term: 1 – 25 years (depending on product)
  • Indicative APR: from 7.34% (Small Business Loan)
  • Time to funding: 5–10 working days
  • Best for: Larger, longer-term or asset-backed borrowing.

Capify

Revenue-based finance repaid as a fixed percentage of daily card takings.

  • Advance amount: £5,000 – £500,000
  • Term: 3 – 12 months typical
  • Cost: Fixed factor rate, no APR
  • Time to funding: 24–72 hours
  • Best for: Retail and hospitality with consistent card revenue.

Tide Business Loans

Loans brokered through Tide's lending marketplace for small businesses.

  • Loan amount: £1,000 – £15m (via partners)
  • Term: 1 month – 6 years
  • Indicative APR: Varies by partner lender
  • Time to funding: From same day
  • Best for: Comparing multiple lenders from one application.

Figures are indicative and may change. Always review each provider's current terms before applying.
