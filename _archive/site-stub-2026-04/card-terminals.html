Card Terminals

Compare leading card machine providers. Review transaction fees, hardware costs, contract terms, and payout speed.

SumUp

Low-cost mobile and countertop card readers with pay-as-you-go pricing.

  • Hardware: SumUp Air, Solo, Solo Lite, 3G+Printer
  • Transaction fee: 1.69% per transaction
  • Monthly fee: £0 (pay-as-you-go) or £19 fixed plan
  • Contract: No contract
  • Best for: Sole traders and small retailers with low volume.

Zettle by PayPal

Simple card readers with integrated POS app and PayPal ecosystem.

  • Hardware: Zettle Reader 2, Terminal
  • Transaction fee: 1.75% per transaction
  • Monthly fee: £0
  • Contract: No contract
  • Best for: Existing PayPal users and small food & retail.

Square

All-in-one card readers, terminals, and free POS software.

  • Hardware: Square Reader, Terminal, Register, Stand
  • Transaction fee: 1.75% in-person, 2.5% keyed
  • Monthly fee: £0 on Free plan
  • Contract: No contract
  • Best for: Hospitality and retail wanting integrated POS.

Stripe Terminal

Developer-friendly in-person payments that integrate with Stripe online.

  • Hardware: BBPOS WisePOS E, Stripe Reader S700
  • Transaction fee: 1.5% + 10p (UK cards)
  • Monthly fee: £0
  • Contract: No contract
  • Best for: Online businesses adding in-person payments.

Worldpay

Enterprise-grade payments from one of the UK's largest acquirers.

  • Hardware: Countertop, portable (Bluetooth), mobile (4G)
  • Transaction fee: From 0.75% (blended, volume-dependent)
  • Monthly fee: £15–£25 typical terminal rental
  • Contract: 12–48 months
  • Best for: Established businesses with higher card volumes.

Dojo

Fast-payout card machines with next-day settlement as standard.

  • Hardware: Dojo Go terminal (4G + Wi-Fi)
  • Transaction fee: From 0.79% + 5p (blended)
  • Monthly fee: £20 terminal rental typical
  • Contract: Rolling monthly
  • Best for: Hospitality wanting fast payouts and tipping flow.

Fees and contract terms vary by business profile. Always request a written quote before signing.
