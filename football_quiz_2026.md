## ROUND 1 (10 questions)

**Q1.** Who scored the fastest hat-trick in Premier League history? Bonus point for the time.
> **Answer:** Sadio Mane — 2 minutes 56 seconds (Southampton vs Aston Villa, 2015)

**Q2.** Who has scored the most own goals in Premier League history?
> **Answer:** Richard Dunne (10 own goals)

**Q3.** Who holds the record for the most Premier League yellow cards?
> **Answer:** Gareth Barry (123 yellow cards)

**Q4.** Which club conceded the fewest goals in a single 38-game Premier League season, what season was it, and how many did they concede? (1 point each)
> **Answer:** Chelsea — 15 goals (2004-05 season, under Jose Mourinho)

**Q5.** Which country has appeared in the most World Cup finals without ever winning the tournament?
> **Answer:** Netherlands (3 finals — 1974, 1978, 2010 — lost all three)

**Q6.** In the 2010 World Cup Final between Spain and the Netherlands, which Dutch player was sent off?
> **Answer:** John Heitinga (second yellow card in extra time)

**Q7.** In the 2019 Champions League Final: (1 point each)
1. Who scored the first goal?
2. Who scored the second goal?
> **Answer:**
> 1. Mohamed Salah (penalty in the 2nd minute)
> 2. Divock Origi (late goal to seal 2-0 for Liverpool vs Tottenham)

**Q8.** Which player scored the most Premier League goals in a single calendar year?
> **Answer:** Harry Kane — 39 goals in 2017

**Q9.** When Leicester won the Premier League in 2016: (1 point each)
1. Who was the Premier League top scorer that season?
2. Which team finished second?
> **Answer:**
> 1. Harry Kane (25 goals)
> 2. Arsenal

**Q10.** What connects: Blackburn Rovers (1995), Leicester City (2016), Nottingham Forest (1978)?
> **Answer:** All won the top division of English football having been promoted from the second tier shortly before.

**Q21.** I'm South African. I scored twice in a Champions League final before I came to England. In the Premier League, I played for Blackburn Rovers and West Ham. I scored 18 league goals in a single season at Blackburn. I later became a first-team coach at Manchester United. Who am I?
