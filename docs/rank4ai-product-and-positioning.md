## 2. The problem we exist to solve

Search has changed shape. Three things are happening simultaneously:

1. **Google AI Overviews now answer ~50% of queries** before a user clicks anything. Brands lose the click even when they "rank #1".
2. **ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Copilot** are becoming primary research tools. They cite a small number of sources per answer — and the citation rules are not the same as Google's ranking rules.
3. **Most SEO advice is wrong for this world.** Keyword density, backlink volume, and meta tag tweaks don't determine whether an LLM will cite you. Entity clarity, structured meaning, and ecosystem validation do.

The outcome: a business can be doing "good SEO" and still be invisible to the systems where buyers now spend their research time.

Rank4AI exists to fix that.

## 5. How it works — the Five Signal Model in plain English

The methodology underneath every audit, deliverable, and retainer.

### Signal 1 — Identity Clarity
Can an AI platform tell, unambiguously, who and what you are? This covers:
- Consistent legal name, brand name, and entity references across every page.
- Organization schema sitewide with sameAs links to Wikidata, Companies House, LinkedIn.
- A clean "About" page that reads like an encyclopaedia entry — because that's what gets extracted.
- The Zero Anaphora Protocol: full entity name on first reference per section, not pronouns. AI passages are extracted out of context, so "we" and "our service" are invisible.

### Signal 2 — Subject Authority
Are you the authority on the topics you sell into? This covers:
- Topic cluster depth (pillar + supporting pages, not isolated articles).
- Named author bylines with Person schema and external authority links.
- Outbound citations to primary sources (yes, outbound — it raises trust).
- Originality: data, frameworks, named methods. AI platforms preferentially cite *named* concepts.

### Signal 3 — Meaning Architecture
Is the content structured so meaning survives extraction? This covers:
- JSON-LD schema as the load-bearing layer (Article, FAQPage, HowTo, Product, Service, Person, Organization).
- Answer-first paragraphs in the first 150 words of every page.
- Definition boxes — the literal text AI platforms quote.
- RAG-ready passages (200–400 word self-contained chunks, no "as mentioned above").
- Heading hierarchy that maps to the Full Prompt Spectrum (what / why / how / when / risks / alternatives).

### Signal 4 — Ecosystem Validation
Does the rest of the internet confirm what your site claims? This covers:
- Wikidata, OpenCorporates, Crunchbase, Companies House registry presence.
- Reviews on Clutch, G2, Trustpilot.
- Co-occurrence with peer entities in industry coverage (AI platforms cluster brands by who is mentioned alongside whom).
- Founder presence on LinkedIn, podcasts, Search Engine Land / Search Engine Journal / Marketing Week.
- GitHub presence for technical credibility.

### Signal 5 — Signal Consistency
Do all the above agree with each other? This is the signal that quietly destroys most brands. It covers:
- Schema text matching visible page text exactly. No drift.
- Brand name spelled the same way on every property.
- Founder name, role, and bio consistent on website, LinkedIn, Companies House, Wikidata.
- One canonical definition per term, used everywhere.
- No competing claims across pages ("we serve enterprise" on one page, "we serve SMBs" on another).

When all five signals are aligned, AI platforms have a stable, confident, citation-ready picture of the brand. When even one is broken, citations decay — usually silently.

## 6. How a client engagement actually runs

A typical journey from first call to outcomes.

**Week 0 — Discovery call (free).** 30 minutes. We diagnose visibility quickly: 10 sample prompts run live against ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity. Most prospects see their absence in real time. This is the moment they buy.

**Weeks 1–3 — Audit.** We run the 50–200 prompt test set, score the Five Signals, build the 17-section report, and present findings on a working call. Client receives the deliverable, the prompt-set, and the prioritised remediation plan.

**Weeks 4–15 — Implementation programme** (optional, but ~70% of audit clients take it). Schema, content, registry alignment, authority build-out. Weekly working sessions with the client's team.

**Month 4 onwards — Retainer** (optional, ~50% conversion from implementation). Ongoing monitoring, drift response, content interventions, quarterly strategy.

**Outcome we sell.** Within 90 days of implementation, AI Inclusion Rate (the % of tracked buyer prompts where the brand is mentioned by name) typically moves from 5–15% to 35–50%. Google AI Overview appearances on tracked queries double or triple. Misclassification drops to near zero. Sales teams start hearing "I asked ChatGPT and it suggested you."
