# Lead-Resale Model + Cross-Sell Map

How a single qualified lead generates multiple revenue events through the in-house phone team. Companion to `docs/fleet-finance-plays.md` (fleet products) and `docs/niche-shortlist-2026-04.md` (master numbered index).

---

## The model in one sentence

**One captured lead → one phone qualification by the in-house team → multiple cash-out events** (lead resale to 3–5 buyers, plus fleet cross-sell, plus affiliate routing).

This is what makes the per-visitor LTV work. It's not the headline £20 PPL — it's the stack.

## Dormant / Resurfacing / Pivoting — definitions for the lead-hunting prompts

These definitions apply across all six portable research prompts in `portable-prompts.md`. Re-read these whenever evaluating a candidate.

### Dormant SERP

A Google UK results page where **3+ of the following** are true:
- Top 10 pages have update dates older than 2 years
- Top 10 includes Reddit / MoneySavingExpert / Mumsnet / forum threads
- Top 10 includes Yell, Yelp, FreeIndex, BT Local, or 2010-era directories
- Top 10 has thin pages (<600 words, no schema)
- No AI Overview, or AI Overview is vague and hedging
- No featured snippet or PAA, or weak PAA
- Same boilerplate template repeats across multiple top-10 results
- Top 10 has low average domain authority

### Resurfacing niche

Search demand was once high, dropped off, and is rising again *now*. Triggers:
- New UK regulation
- New trend or cultural moment
- Demographic catching up (e.g. Gen X estate planning, adult ADHD diagnosis)
- Taboo lifting (e.g. men's mental health, TRT)
- Technology making an old product viable again

### Pivoting niche

Buyers' needs are shifting *now* because of:
- New compliance forcing change (FHL tax abolition, Schedule 23)
- Old product being phased out (boilers → heat pumps, ASTs → APTs)
- New format winning (WordPress → Webflow, photographers → AI generators)
- Audience moving (Gen Z weddings, side-hustle economy)

### What disqualifies a niche

- Top 10 has 5+ updated, schema-rich pages from authority brands → too competitive
- Aggressive AI Overview citing 5+ named brands → consensus formed, skip
- YMYL with hard regulatory walls (FCA / MHRA / GDC / GMC / SRA) → skip unless affiliate path bypasses
- Property finance — dropped from this plan
- Single-buyer markets where leads can only sell once

## The promise to the user (printed above every form on every site)

The in-house phone team only delivers the model if the visitor experience matches the promise. Lead-gen forms across **FindATradey** and **FindAGym** must show this block visibly before the form submits:

> **Three vetted local providers. No spam. We don't sell your number to dozens of contractors.**
> All providers verified weekly against the relevant register (Gas Safe / NICEIC / OFTEC / MCS / CQC / Ofsted / FSA — depending on the site).
> Free for you. Providers pay only when they win the job.
> Once the job's done, we don't ring you again.

Without that promise visibly enforced, the conversion rate halves and the lead-resale model collapses into a Bark-clone. The promise is the whole moat.
