## Status legend

- **Active** — currently in build sequence

- **Phase 2** — kept alive, scheduled later

- **Dropped** — formally removed from the plan (kept on record only)

## Tag legend

- **QW** — Quick-win (4–12 weeks to first measurable result)
- **MT** — Mid-term (3–9 months)
- **LT** — Long-term (9–24 months)
- **WL** — White-label embed (own product alongside content)

- **REG** — Heavier regulation (FCA / MHRA / SRA / GDC)

## Pattern legend

- **D** — Dormant SERP
- **R** — Resurfacing niche
- **P** — Pivoting niche
- **L** — Long-tail aggregation
- **H** — Hyperlocal programmatic
- **F** — Fleet-aligned (sells own product)
- **T** — Time-boxed (recency window)
- **A** — Anti-niche / competitor traffic

## Strategy at a glance

Three revenue layers stacked on the same content empire:

1. **Big-ticket — sell own products** through the existing fleet (loans, IF, terminals, agency) plus a new tech directory.
2. **Mid-ticket — lead resale to multiple buyers** through dormant / pivoting / resurfacing programmatic sites. Each lead qualified once by the in-house phone team and resold to 3–5 buyers.
3. **Low-ticket / volume — affiliate + AdSense** through quick-CPL tools, white-label products, and long-tail aggregation matrices.

See `docs/reference/lead-resale-model.md` for the multi-buyer routing and cross-sell map.

## Reference data (April 2026 — used to justify scoring decisions)

- AI Overviews: 88% cite 3+ sources; 46.5% of cited URLs rank outside top 50; YouTube ≈ 18% of citations from outside top 100
- Pages with 15+ recognised entities ≈ 4.8× more likely cited; high semantic completeness ≈ 4.2×; multimodal ≈ 1.5× higher selection
- 46% of AIO appearances on 7+ word queries; 58% are question-form
- Zero-click rate on AIO-triggered searches ≈ 83%; AIOs reduce CTR by ≈ 58%
- Cited pages ≈ 35% more organic clicks, 91% more paid clicks vs uncited
- Wikipedia ≈ 7.8% of all ChatGPT citations; Reddit ≈ 6.6% of Perplexity citations
- 12.2 million UK gym members 2026 (record); 18% of over-16s
- 2nd-charge new business +27% YoY 2025–26; avg loan £51k; 35.2% home-improvement-purpose
- UK avg wedding 2026: £24,747 (down 3.6% YoY); avg venue £6,040 ex-cat / £9,695 inc-cat; London +35–40% vs national
- UK SME private-label pet supplement: 2-week launch (Aviform), 70–80% margin typical
- MTD ITSA: April 2026 first wave for sole traders + landlords ≥ £50k income
- BUS uplift: £9,000 for oil/LPG homes from July 2026
- WCS: £500/socket from April 2026, scheme ends 31 March 2027
- Renters' Rights Act: tenancy reforms 1 May 2026; AST → APT conversion forced
