## Route 3 — Direct CPL deals (highest payout per lead, slowest to set up)

Once you have any traffic, ring up the broker / acquirer / lender directly
and negotiate per-lead pricing. Often pays £50–£500 per qualified lead vs
£20–£100 from a public affiliate programme.

**Targets to call once traffic exists:**

- **Brokers** — Capitalise, Funding Options, Swoop, Rangewell, Rise
  Funding. They take any qualified loan applicant and can pay cleaner CPL
  than chasing 14 lenders direct.
- **Card terminal ISOs / brokers** — Take Payments, Paymentsense, MerchantSavvy,
  CardSwitcher, Boon Pay. They handle the acquirer relationships and pay
  per merchant signed up.
- **Invoice finance brokers** — Wise Factoring, FundInvoice, Touch Financial.
- **High-risk merchant specialists** — Trust Payments, Universe Payments,
  Acquired.com, RYFT, PayDock — these are where the £200–£500 CPLs live.

---

## Route 4 — Build the broker (the moat play)

Long-term: get FCA-authorised as a credit broker (for loans) or
PSR-registered as a payments introducer, and capture the full
commission yourself instead of giving the broker a cut.

- **Loans:** FCA limited permission for credit broking — paperwork-heavy
  but achievable in 8–12 weeks.
- **Card terminals:** Often no licensing needed for introducer; ISO
  status with an acquirer is the bigger play.
- **Cost:** ~£1.5k–£3k application fees + ongoing reporting overhead.

This is where the maths stops being £50/lead and starts being £500–£2k+
per signed merchant or rev-share on processing volume. Defer until
traffic justifies it, but it's the right end-state.

## Affiliate-friendly niche ideas — easy money, short cycles

Some niches monetise faster than others because the buyer journey is
short, signup is self-serve, and approval is quick. Prioritise these
when you want quick affiliate revenue:

| Niche | Why easy | Affiliate path |
|---|---|---|
| **Business bank accounts** (best for X, switching to Tide/Starling) | Self-serve signup, 10-min onboarding, payout in days | Awin: Tide, Starling, Revolut |
| **Card terminals for [trade]** | Self-serve, instant device order, no contract | Direct: SumUp, Square, Zettle |
| **Tap to Pay on iPhone** | Software-only, instant activation | Direct: Stripe, Square, SumUp, Zettle |
| **Website builders for [vertical]** | Self-serve trial → paid plan | Direct: Wix, Squarespace, Shopify |
| **Hosting for [vertical]** | Self-serve signup | Direct: Bluehost, SiteGround, Hostinger |

| **Business insurance** (Superscript, Simply Business) | Quote-and-buy in minutes | Awin / direct |

## Cookie windows / attribution — what to look for

- **30 days** is the standard. Anything less, push back.
- **60–90 days** is good for high-consideration verticals (loans).
- **First-click vs last-click** matters — direct programmes often
  give first-click, networks often last-click. Read the T&Cs.
- **Cross-device tracking** — Awin and Impact handle it; some direct
  programmes don't.
- **Validation periods** — lenders can hold commission 30–90 days while
  the customer "sticks". Plan cash flow around it.
