# Regulatory notes — UK SMB lead-gen

Working assumption. Sanity-check with a regulatory solicitor before launch
(30-min call, £150–300, takes a day to book), but this is the operating
position.

## FCA / Consumer Credit — when we need it

We don't — **provided we restrict to unregulated lending only**.

UK consumer credit regulation (FCA / Consumer Credit Act) catches lending
to "individuals". Under the Act, "individuals" includes sole traders and
small partnerships (≤3 partners). Lending to **limited companies, LLPs,
and partnerships of 4+ is unregulated**. There's also an exemption
(Article 60C(5) RAO) for business loans over £25k to sole traders or
small partnerships wholly or predominantly for business purposes — also
unregulated.

If FundBiz only refers business borrowers in the unregulated space, no
FCA credit-broker permission is required.

## Operational rule for FundBiz / lead-gen

- **Eligible structures:** Ltd, PLC, LLP, partnerships of 4+.
- **Ineligible:** sole traders, partnerships of ≤3.
- Eligibility checker filters by company structure on question 1.
- Sole traders are directed to Start Up Loans / British Business Bank.
- T&Cs state B2B / limited-company-only.
- Marketing copy speaks to "limited company directors" and "your
  business", not "you" as an individual.

## What we ask in forms

- Director name, business email, company name, registration number
  (Companies House), business sector, turnover band, loan amount,
  loan purpose.
- No personal data beyond what's needed to qualify a business.
- Don't advise on personal guarantees — refer to lender's PG terms.
- Don't quote or guarantee approval — we're an introducer.

## Financial promotion rules (Section 21 FSMA) — apply anyway

Even unregulated lending content must be clear, fair, not misleading.
Fee transparency, APRs disclosed where stated, no exaggerated approval
claims, paid relationships declared on editorial pages ("we may earn a
commission").

## Lender affiliate programme T&Cs

Most major UK SMB lender programmes (iwoca, Funding Circle, Capify,
Liberis, YouLend, Capital on Tap, Tide) accept ltd-only intermediaries
without requiring FCA authorisation. **Confirm per programme** before
signing. Networks (Awin, Impact) may have their own vetting — not
typically a blocker.

## Future regulatory risk

HMT has been consulting on whether to extend regulation to SME lending
generally. 12–24 month horizon, not a today problem. Track but don't
gate on.

## When we'd want FCA voluntarily later

- Cross-sell into consumer-side products (director loans, personal
  liability cards)
- Use FCA-authorised status as a brand trust signal (Capitalise,
  Funding Options, Swoop all do this prominently)
- Accept sole trader applicants
- Move from introducer into advisory territory

## CardMachines — separate regime

Card terminals / merchant acquiring is regulated under the Payment
Services Regulations 2017, but the regulation falls on the *acquirer*,
not on the introducer. CardMachines as a lead-gen / referral site
does not need PSR registration. ISO status with an acquirer (rather
than basic introducer) unlocks higher commissions (£500+ per signed
merchant vs £100 per lead) but isn't required for the basic model.

## BBL — clean editorial

Pure editorial / review content with no application forms sits outside
the regulatory perimeter. Financial promotion rules still apply
(paid-relationship disclosure on pages where applicable).

## Net effect on the build plan

- FCA was 🔴 Critical in the synthesis. **Reclassified to 🟢 Worth
  tracking.**
- FundBiz application forms are NOT gated by an 8–12 week regulatory
  process.
- We can ship lead capture from day one, restricted to unregulated
  lending.
- The 30/60/90 plan can parallel-ship FundBiz post-decline matchers
  with CardMachines `/high-risk/`.
