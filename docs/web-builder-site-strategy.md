# Rank4AI Web Builder — Site Strategy

**Site:** the marketing site for Rank4AI's DIY AI-ready web builder (Lovable-style — users describe their business, the builder generates a Five-Signal-compliant site).
**Goal:** Rank fast in Google SEO, Google AI Overviews, and AI Search to drive sign-ups and self-serve revenue.
**Framework alignment:** Marketing site is itself a reference implementation of the Five Signal Model — it is the proof of product.

---

## 1. Positioning

**One-line positioning.** The only website builder that ships sites AI platforms can actually find, understand, and recommend.

**Why this wins fast.**
- Lovable, Wix, Framer, Webflow, Squarespace, 10Web, Durable all compete on "build a beautiful site fast." None compete on "build a site that ranks in AI search." That category is empty.
- We have a defensible methodology (the Five Signal Model) that converts directly into product features.
- "AI website builder" has high commercial-intent search volume; "AI-ready website builder" has near-zero competition. We own the second and steal share from the first.

**Audiences.**
1. Founders of small businesses, agencies, freelancers building client sites — non-technical, want fast.
2. Solopreneurs, coaches, consultants who need a credible online presence visible in ChatGPT.
3. Side-project builders coming from Lovable / v0 / Bolt who want a marketing site that actually drives traffic.

**Buying triggers.**
- "I built a site and nobody finds it."
- "ChatGPT recommends my competitor, not me."
- "I don't know what schema is and I don't want to."
- "Wix/Squarespace doesn't get my brand into AI answers."

## 4. What goes on each page (page templates)

Three repeatable templates cover the whole site.

### Template 1 — "Vs. competitor" page (Cluster B)
- H1: "{Builder} vs Rank4AI Builder: Which builds an AI-ready website?"
- Answer-first paragraph: 1-line winner + reason (within 150 words).
- Comparison table: 12 rows × 2 cols. Rows = Five Signal capabilities (auto-schema, llms.txt, RAG-ready output, AI visibility monitoring, identity clarity controls, etc.).
- "When to choose {Builder}" honest section — keeps trust + ranks for branded queries.
- "When to choose us" — clear conversion section.
- Migration CTA: "Move from {Builder} in one click."
- FAQ block (5 Qs) marked up with FAQPage schema.
- Schema: WebPage + FAQPage + ComparisonTable (custom).

### Template 2 — Industry / use-case template (Clusters D + E)
- H1: "Build a {industry} website with AI built in."
- Live preview of a generated template for that industry.
- "What a great {industry} website needs" (8 bullets, AI-extractable).
- "How AI platforms describe {industry} businesses" — angle nobody else has.
- Five-Signal checklist filtered for the industry.
- 3 example sites built with the tool (case-study cards).
- FAQ block (5 Qs) including the "is this good for SEO?" / "will ChatGPT recommend my {industry} business?" questions.
- Schema: WebPage + Product + FAQPage + ItemList (templates).

### Template 3 — Platform / education / tools (Clusters C, F, G)
- H1: clear question or imperative.
- Definition box (canonical 2–3 sentence answer).
- TL;DR list (5 bullets).
- Body in Full Prompt Spectrum order (what / why / how / when / risks / alternatives).
- Embedded free tool (Cluster F) or live demo (Cluster C) — drives sign-ups.
- FAQ + related links.
- Schema: WebPage + FAQPage + HowTo (where applicable) + SoftwareApplication (tool pages).

**Universal page elements (all templates):**
- Sitewide Organization + WebSite + BreadcrumbList schema.
- Author byline (Adam Parker or named team member) with Person schema.
- Last-updated date, prominent.
- One specific CTA (sign up, run the tool, see pricing).
- Outbound citations to 2–3 authoritative sources.
- llms.txt entry pointing to the page if it's a high-priority asset.

**Days 1–14 — Foundations + conversion core.**
- Cluster A in full (homepage, how-it-works, features, pricing, sign-up).

**Days 15–45 — Commercial intent.**
- All 8 Cluster B "vs. competitor" pages — these convert fastest because intent is highest.
- All 6 Cluster C platform pages — instant differentiation from every other builder.
- Three more free tools (Cluster F) — each one a Product Hunt-able asset.

**Days 46–90 — Traffic engine.**
- 16 industry template pages (Cluster D) — long-tail volume.

**Why this order:** Conversion-ready pages first (we capture the small but high-intent traffic immediately while the long-tail engine warms up). The traffic engine then feeds into already-built conversion pages.

## 9. Risks & guardrails

- **Comparison pages must stay honest.** Misrepresenting competitors invites legal + reputational hits and AI platforms penalise contradicted claims. Cite competitor docs directly.
- **Don't ship 60 thin pages.** Each page must hit the template depth. Better to ship 30 strong pages in month 1 and 30 more in month 2 than 60 thin ones day one.
- **Templates must be genuinely different per industry.** Duplicate-content collision will kill ranking. Each template page needs unique copy, unique screenshots, unique FAQs.
- **Free tools must be useful, not lead-bait.** A weak tool damages Subject Authority. Build fewer, better.
- **Footer "built with" backlink** must be `rel="dofollow"` from free plans, optional on paid. Disclose clearly.
- **Founder-led launch.** Adam Parker fronts the launch (X, LinkedIn, podcasts). Founder-led product launches in this category outperform faceless launches by an order of magnitude.
