# Rank4AI Site Strategy

**Site:** rank4ai.co.uk
**Product:** Done-for-you AI Search optimisation (AI SEO) consultancy and audits
**Goal:** Rank fast in Google SEO, Google AI Overviews, and AI Search (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot) to drive qualified inbound for audits and retainers.
**Framework alignment:** Every page is built to the Rank4AI Five Signal Model (Identity Clarity, Subject Authority, Meaning Architecture, Ecosystem Validation, Signal Consistency).

---

## 1. Positioning

**One-line positioning.** Rank4AI is the UK consultancy that gets your business cited and recommended by AI platforms — not just ranked on Google.

**Why this wins fast.** "AI SEO" / "AI search optimisation" / "GEO" / "AEO" are emerging categories with low keyword difficulty and rapidly rising volume. Most incumbents are repositioned SEO agencies; Rank4AI owns a proprietary methodology (Five Signal Model + 17-section audit), which is a defensible authority moat.

**Audiences.**
1. Founders / CMOs at B2B SaaS, professional services, agencies (£1M–£50M turnover) seeing organic traffic erode.
2. Heads of SEO inside larger orgs needing a vendor that speaks AI search fluently.
3. In-house marketers wanting a defensible audit they can present internally.

**Buying triggers.**
- "Our brand isn't appearing in ChatGPT / Google AI Overviews."
- "Organic traffic is down because AI Overviews answer the query."
- "We need to prove to the board we're future-proofed."

## 4. What goes on each page (Five-Signal page template)

Every page is built to the same template so the site reads as a coherent entity to AI crawlers.

1. **H1 with primary entity + intent verb.** ("How to optimise for ChatGPT", not "ChatGPT SEO Tips").
2. **Answer-first paragraph in first 150 words.** Direct answer to the query, no preamble. Full entity name on first reference (Zero Anaphora Protocol).
3. **Definition box.** A 2–3 sentence canonical definition that AI platforms can extract verbatim.
4. **TL;DR / key takeaways list.** Five bullets, parseable as a passage.
5. **Structured body** with H2/H3 hierarchy mapping to the Full Prompt Spectrum:
   - What is it?
   - How does it work?
   - Why does it matter?
   - When/where does it apply?
   - How do I do it? (steps)
   - Risks / limitations.
   - Comparisons / alternatives.
6. **Evidence block.** Named data, figures, citations to primary sources (not "studies show").
7. **FAQ block** (5–8 Qs) — directly answers People Also Ask + AI prompt variants. Marked up with FAQPage schema.
8. **Comparison table** where relevant (e.g. ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini sourcing).
9. **Related entities sidebar / inline links** — internal links to 4–6 cluster siblings using exact entity names.
10. **Author byline + reviewer + last-updated date.** Person schema, sameAs to LinkedIn.
11. **Schema stack:** Organization (sitewide), WebPage, Article (or HowTo / FAQPage / Product / Service as fits), BreadcrumbList, Person.
12. **Outbound citations** to 2–3 authoritative external sources per page (builds Ecosystem Validation).
13. **Single, specific CTA** matched to page intent (audit, demo, download).

## 6. Fast-traffic sequencing (first 90 days)

Build order optimised for fastest indexation and AI inclusion.

**Days 1–14 — Foundations.**
- Identity pages: `/`, `/about`, `/about/adam-parker`, `/methodology`, `/the-five-signal-model`.
- Sitewide schema, llms.txt, robots.txt for LLMs, XML sitemap.
- Wikidata + Companies House alignment.

**Days 15–45 — Pillars + glossary.**
- All six pillar pages (Clusters A–F).
- Glossary (one canonical entry per term — the page AI extracts from).
- Six platform-specific pages (Cluster C) — these get cited inside the platforms they target.

**Days 46–90 — Cluster fill-in + verticals.**
- Remaining cluster children.
- Industry verticals (Cluster E) — long-tail commercial traffic.
- Three case studies + reviews live on Clutch / G2.

**Why this order:** AI platforms need to identify the entity before they cite it. Identity + pillars first, depth second, verticals third.

## 8. Risks & guardrails

- **Don't over-publish.** 55 deeply-built pages beats 200 thin pages. Thin pages dilute Subject Authority.
- **No content collision.** Each page owns one primary question. Quarterly collision scan.
- **Terminology lock.** "AI Search Optimisation" is the parent term. GEO/AEO/LLMO are sub-terms, never used interchangeably. Drift kills Signal Consistency.
- **Schema-content parity.** Visible text must match schema verbatim. Mismatches are penalised.
- **Founder-led, not faceless.** Adam Parker is the named author/reviewer on every authority page. AI platforms cite people, not anonymous brands.
