# FundBiz — competitive deep-dive

Last updated: 2026-04-25
Site: FundBiz — UK business loans broker / transactional lead-gen, with
permission to flex into adjacent SMB finance (cards, bank accounts, MCA,
asset finance, insurance).
Sister site: BBL (BestBusinessLoans) — pure editorial / review site.

---

## 1. Executive summary

**Where FundBiz plays.** Transactional UK SMB finance — application
forms, eligibility checkers, calculators, sector landers and post-decline
matchers. Sweet-spot ticket £1k–£250k working capital, growth, MCA, and
"less-than-perfect-credit" loans. Monetised via direct CPL with iwoca,
Funding Circle, Capify, 365, Liberis, YouLend, plus Awin for ancillary
products (Tide, Capital on Tap, Superscript).

**How it differs from BBL.** BBL is the editorial / "best X for Y"
review site that wins on AI citations and informational SERPs — no
forms, no eligibility tools, doesn't drift off loans. FundBiz is the
*funnel*: it owns the bottom-of-funnel "apply / get matched" intent
that BBL refuses to host, and is the only network site licensed to
flex into adjacent SMB finance (cards, accounts, insurance, asset
finance, MCA) when the buyer's already on a loans journey. BBL feeds
FundBiz on internal links from "best [X] loan" reviews into FundBiz
matchers and applications.

**Top opportunity.** Post-decline ("rejected for a business loan",
"loan with CCJ", "loan with bad credit"). The Bank Referral Scheme
hands designated platforms (Funding Options/Tide, Alternative
Business Funding, Funding Xchange) the official referral pipe — but
the SEO/AI-Overview surface for those queries is dominated by direct
lenders (JPM Capital, Bizcap, Acorn, Bolton Finance) and one strong
broker page (Funding Options' "Business finance with a CCJ"). No-one
owns the **decline-by-reason** matcher (CCJ, missed payments, thin
file, pre-revenue, sub-12-month trading). That's FundBiz's wedge:
diagnose-the-decline → match-to-product → forms. Direct CPL with
specialist MCA / RBF lenders (Liberis, YouLend, 365, Capify) lands
£100–£300/lead. Target 25 prioritised pages in section 6 below.

### Headline read

- **Rangewell is the only competitor doing sector landers properly.**
  Everyone else relies on the knowledge hub / blog and a generic loans
  page. That's a weakness because Rangewell's UX is dated and the
  conversion path is callback-only — FundBiz can out-convert with
  modern application forms and calculators on the same sector
  keywords.
- **15-industry FundBiz wedge:** build modern sector landers for
  hospitality, pubs, restaurants, construction, e-commerce, retail,
  beauty, gyms, healthcare (dental, vet, pharmacy), transport,
  recruitment (with invoice-finance handoff), agriculture, franchises,
  manufacturing sub-niches, and professional services. Each lander =
  product matcher + sector-specific eligibility + sector-specific FAQ
  + calculator. ~15 pages, ~12 of which are wide-open.
- **E-commerce is the standout gap.** No competitor has a clean
  e-commerce / online-retail loan lander, despite it being the natural
  home for MCA / RBF (Liberis, YouLend, 365). Direct CPL is highest
  here.
- **Recruitment is the specialist gap.** Single decent US lander
  (Swoop) and nothing strong on the UK side; recruitment agencies are
  invoice-finance heavy — perfect cross-link to MarketInvoice (sister
  site) and direct-CPL with Sonovate.

## 4. Post-decline opportunity

### Size

- **40–56% of UK SME bank loan applications are declined** (UK Finance,
  Ipsos SME Finance Monitor, money.co.uk, Allica research, all 2024–25
  reads). Success rate fell from ~80% in 2018 to ~50% in 2024.
- **£22bn–£65bn SME credit gap** (Allica / British Business Bank
  research). Half of declined SMEs don't reapply elsewhere.
- **~111,000 cumulative referrals** through the Bank Referral Scheme
  to Q3 2024 (~15–20k/year incremental). HMT consultation in spring
  2026 may expand this.
- **Search volume signal:** "business loan with bad credit",
  "business loan with CCJ", "business loan rejected", "business loan
  declined" — each in the 500–2,000 monthly UK searches range with
  high commercial intent. Long tail (`+ "limited company"`,
  `+ "sole trader"`, `+ "first year of trading"`, `+ "missed payments"`)
  multiplies that 4–6×.
- **Conservative TAM model:** 15% of declined SMEs search before
  reapplying ≈ 50–80k high-intent search journeys/year. At a 3% capture
  to FundBiz application × £150 average direct CPL = **£225k–£360k/year
  of revenue** at modest market share, before MCA / RBF upsell.

### Ownership today

| Query / surface | Who owns it | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| "business loan with bad credit" | money.co.uk, Bionic, ABF, JPM Capital, Bizcap, Acorn Finance | Mixed — informational money.co.uk + lender direct pages. No clean broker matcher. |
| "business loan with CCJ" | Funding Options (knowledge hub), Capalona, Rangewell, Bolton Finance, Aurora Capital | Funding Options article wins citations; Capalona has a transactional page; Rangewell has a sector-style "/business-finance-ccjs" |
| "business loan rejected / declined" | British Business Bank, Capitalise (insights), Rise Funding, money.co.uk | Pure informational; **no-one runs a "diagnose your decline → match" funnel** |
| Bank Referral Scheme | Funding Options, Funding Xchange, ABF (mandated platforms) | Locked — government-designated. FundBiz cannot get on the list short-term. |
| "alternative to bank loan" | Funding Circle, iwoca (lender direct), Funding Options | Lender-direct rather than broker-matched |
| AI Overviews on these queries | British Business Bank + Funding Options dominate today | Informational pages winning the cite — broker matchers losing |

### The gap

No competitor runs a **diagnose-the-decline matcher**. The shape is:

> "Why were you turned down?" (radio: CCJ / missed payments / thin file
> / pre-revenue / sub-12-month trading / poor business performance /
> personal credit / unsecured-collateral missing) → reason-specific
> explainer page → product matcher (asset finance vs MCA vs RBF vs
> secured vs CDFI vs Start Up Loan) → application form for the
> appropriate lender(s).

Funding Options has the best single article. Rangewell has a CCJ
sector page. Nobody has the **interactive matcher** by reason of
decline. That is the wedge.

1. **`/declined/`** — top-of-funnel hub: "Declined for a business loan?
    Here's what to do next." Diagnose-the-decline interactive matcher
    (radio buttons → reason → product). Targets head term `business
    loan rejected` and `business loan declined`. Primary surface: SEO +
    AI Overviews (clean question/answer structure). Internal links out
    to all 4 pages below.
2. **`/loans/with-ccj/`** — "Business loans with a CCJ in 2026."
    Targets `business loan with ccj`, `business finance ccj`. Includes
    satisfied vs unsatisfied CCJ table, lender criteria, asset finance
    + invoice finance + secured loan options. Direct CPL: 365, Capify,
    YouLend, Liberis. Primary surface: SEO + AI Overviews.
3. **`/loans/bad-credit/`** — "Business loans with bad credit." Targets
    `business loan bad credit`, `bad credit business loan uk`. Volume
    leader of the four. Primary surface: SEO. CPL: same MCA/RBF panel.
4. **`/loans/missed-payments-mortgage-arrears/`** — niched and underserved.
    Targets `business loan missed payments`, `business loan mortgage
    arrears`. Primary surface: AI Search (citation play — almost no-one
    writes for this). CPL: secured / asset finance lenders.
5. **`/loans/first-year-trading/`** — pre-revenue / sub-12-month

    AI Overviews. Direct routes: Start Up Loans (British Business

Each page must have: clean H2-as-question structure, FAQPage schema,
named author, in-page calculator showing repayment for the typical
post-decline product (MCA factor rate / asset finance / secured), and
a "diagnose your decline" widget cross-linking to `/declined/`.

### Flex rules

- **Don't replicate BBL.** No "best [X] for [Y]" listicles. FundBiz
  flex pages must have a transactional element (form, calculator,
  matcher, eligibility check). Pure listicles go to BBL.
- **Don't replicate MarketInvoice or CardMachines.** When the buyer
  needs invoice finance or a card terminal, hand off to the sister
  site. Don't fight your own network.
- **The 80/20 split:** ~80% of FundBiz pages are direct loans / MCA /
  asset finance / post-decline. Up to 20% flex into cards / accounts /
  insurance / VAT loans / R&D / grants — only when the buyer is
  already on a loans journey.

## 7. Open questions

1. **FCA limited permission for credit broking.** Required before
   running application forms with attribution. Route-to-market doc
   says ~8–12 weeks and ~£1.5–3k. Need to start the application now or
   it gates pages 1–5 from going live. Confirm: do we hold permission
   already, or kick off?
2. **Direct CPL contracts.** Liberis, YouLend, 365 and Capify all run
   direct partner programmes — but most require traffic before they
   sign. What's the minimum-traffic threshold for each, and which can
   we sign on referral / soft-launch? Capify's affiliate is direct and
   instant per route-to-market doc; 365 the same. Confirm Liberis +
   YouLend.
3. **HMT Bank Referral Scheme consultation (spring 2026).** Treasury is
   consulting on expanding designated platforms beyond Funding
   Options / ABF / Funding Xchange. If FundBiz were eligible to apply,
   that would change the post-decline economics materially. Worth
   tracking the consultation timeline.
4. **Cross-link governance with BBL.** BBL's "best [X] for [Y]"
   editorial pages need to internal-link into FundBiz application
   forms, but the two sites must stay editorially independent for the
   E-E-A-T story to work. Define the linking pattern (footer? in-line
   CTA only? lender-card "apply" link?).
5. **Sister-site overlap on MCA.** BBL also writes about MCA. Confirm
   the rule: BBL = "best MCA providers" review listicle; FundBiz =
   MCA application matcher + sector pages. No duplication.
6. **Schema strategy.** Skipped per scope, but will be load-bearing on
   AIO pages (FAQPage, HowTo for "what to do after decline", FinancialProduct
   for each loan type). Pick this up in follow-up.
7. **Calculator engineering.** £1k–£250k loan calc, MCA factor-rate
   calc, asset finance amortisation calc. Build once, reuse across
   sector pages. Buy vs build (e.g. embedded Capitalise / Funding
   Options widgets vs in-house React)?
8. **Trustpilot / review-collection plan.** Funding Options, Capitalise
   and Capify all run high-volume Trustpilot collection. FundBiz needs
   a review-collection programme from day one — this is an entity-graph
   trust signal AI Search rewards heavily.
9. **Atlas Credit ambiguity.** Several "Atlas" UK finance entities
   exist (Atlas Trade Finance, Atlas Business Finance, Atlas Finance
   Scotland). Confirm which one was meant in the brief — fragmented
   entity graph means none is a strong competitor individually.
10. **Spanish / Polish / Bengali UK community gap on post-decline
    content.** Per niche-brief Fleet rules, multilingual goes to Fleet
    not FundBiz — but the post-decline Polish-builder / Bengali-grocer
    audience is huge and underserved. Worth a follow-up brief on which
    Fleet microsite catches it.
