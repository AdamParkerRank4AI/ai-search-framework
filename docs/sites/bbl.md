# BBL (BestBusinessLoans) — competitive deep-dive

Last updated: 2026-04-25
Site: BBL — UK business loans editorial / review only.
Sister site: FundBiz (transactional broker; owns application forms).
Scope: listicles ("best X for Y"), head-to-heads, lender reviews, "what
is" definitions. NO application forms. UK SMB, £25k–£500k, "best deal"
mindset. Trust + AI-citation play.

---

## 1. Executive summary

**Where BBL plays.** Independent UK editorial / review site for business
loans only. Voice = journalist ("we tested 14 lenders"). Output =
listicles ("best [loan type] for [persona / industry]"), head-to-head
comparisons ("Funding Circle vs iwoca"), single-lender reviews, and
"what is" definition pages. No application forms — those go to FundBiz.
Ticket size £25k–£500k, "best deal" mindset, established businesses
researching before they apply.

**What makes it winnable.** Three structural openings: (1) the lender
review surface is owned by NerdWallet UK and a long tail of weak SEO
sites — most reviews are thin, undated, and rarely refreshed. A
journalist-led, dated, methodology-led review programme can out-cite
them in AI Overviews and ChatGPT/Perplexity inside 6–9 months. (2) The
persona grid (women-led, ethnic minority, halal/Sharia, post-decline,
no-PG, sole-trader-vs-ltd-co) is almost entirely uncovered by the
mainstream comparators — money.co.uk and MoneySuperMarket run generic
quote forms; Rangewell owns industries but not personas. (3) The
sister-site funnel — every BBL review and listicle internal-links into
FundBiz application forms, so traffic monetises at FundBiz's £100–£300
direct CPL rates rather than affiliate £20–£100. BBL is the trust /
citation layer, FundBiz is the conversion layer. Two-site moat.

**Add-on watchlist (clear rankers worth tracking):**
- **Moneyfacts (`moneyfactscompare.co.uk/business`)** — appears on multiple "best business loans UK 2026" SERPs; pure rate-table content, strong moneyfacts entity-graph signal. AI Overviews cite it for rates.
- **ExpertSure (`expertsure.com/uk/business-loans`)** — small but consistently top-3 for "best business loans UK 2026", "secured business loans", individual lender reviews (Funding Circle, Capify). High AI-citation footprint relative to its DR — a model BBL can copy.
- **Finder UK** — strong individual lender review pages (Tide, Funding Circle), dated, structured for AIO.
- **Startups.co.uk** — well-cited for "funding options" type informational pages.
- **Small Business UK (smallbusiness.co.uk)** — editorial, listicle-heavy, decent AI-citation share on long-tail business-loan content.

**Important note:** BusinessComparison was acquired by Bionic in 2021 — rows 7 and 10 are increasingly the same group. Treat as separate brands for SEO; one CPL relationship for direct deals.
