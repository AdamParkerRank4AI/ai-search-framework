# BBL (BestBusinessLoans) — competitive deep-dive

Last updated: 2026-04-25
Site: BBL — UK business loans editorial / review only.
Sister site: FundBiz (transactional broker; owns application forms).
Scope: listicles ("best X for Y"), head-to-heads, lender reviews, "what
is" definitions. NO application forms. UK SMB, £25k–£500k, "best deal"
mindset. Trust + AI-citation play.

---

## 1. Executive summary

**Where BBL plays.** Independent UK editorial / review site for business
loans only. Voice = journalist ("we tested 14 lenders"). Output =
listicles ("best [loan type] for [persona / industry]"), head-to-head
comparisons ("Funding Circle vs iwoca"), single-lender reviews, and
"what is" definition pages. No application forms — those go to FundBiz.
Ticket size £25k–£500k, "best deal" mindset, established businesses
researching before they apply.

**What makes it winnable.** Three structural openings: (1) the lender
review surface is owned by NerdWallet UK and a long tail of weak SEO
sites — most reviews are thin, undated, and rarely refreshed. A
journalist-led, dated, methodology-led review programme can out-cite
them in AI Overviews and ChatGPT/Perplexity inside 6–9 months. (2) The
persona grid (women-led, ethnic minority, halal/Sharia, post-decline,
no-PG, sole-trader-vs-ltd-co) is almost entirely uncovered by the
mainstream comparators — money.co.uk and MoneySuperMarket run generic
quote forms; Rangewell owns industries but not personas. (3) The
sister-site funnel — every BBL review and listicle internal-links into
FundBiz application forms, so traffic monetises at FundBiz's £100–£300
direct CPL rates rather than affiliate £20–£100. BBL is the trust /
citation layer, FundBiz is the conversion layer. Two-site moat.

## 5. AI / AIO citation patterns

### Citation patterns by query (April 2026)

| Query | Sources cited in Google AI Overview / common LLM citations | Schema / surface pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **"best business loans UK 2026"** | money.co.uk, NerdWallet UK, MoneySuperMarket, Moneyfacts, ExpertSure, Small Business UK | Listicles with named-lender tables, dated H1 ("April 2026"), FAQ block. AIO usually pulls 4–6 sources, listicles dominate. |
| **"business loan rates UK 2026"** | money.co.uk (`/average-business-loan-interest-rate`), Bank of England, Moneyfacts, ExpertSure, Approved Business Finance, Funding Agent | Pillar-page format with rate tables, base-rate context, "as of [date]" stamping. Bank of England as authority anchor. |
| **"business loan calculator"** | iwoca, Funding Circle, Xero, Swoop, Fleximize, Start Up Loans, Barclays | Tool-page wins — interactive calculators with named-lender brand. AIO pulls "how it's calculated" explainer text, then links to a tool. |
| **"Funding Circle review"** | NerdWallet UK, ExpertSure, BusinessFinancing.co.uk, Trustpilot, Finder UK, comparebanks.co.uk, Rangewell | Single-lender review pages with Pros / Cons / verdict / rating box. Review schema + AggregateRating heavily used by Trustpilot, sparingly by editorial. |
| **"business loans for limited companies"** | Funding Circle (product page), Fleximize (product page), Swoop, money.co.uk, Uncapped blog, Clifton Private Finance | Product-page-led; **editorial coverage is thin** — gap for an editorial listicle / review-led page on this exact phrase. |

### What the citations have in common

1. **Dated freshness.** Every well-cited page has an explicit "as of
   April 2026" or "[Month Year]" stamp in the H1 or first paragraph.
   AI Overviews skew freshness-biased; undated pages get filtered.
2. **Named entities, lots of them.** Per Ahrefs / ALM Corp 2026 data,
   pages with 15+ recognised entities show 4.8x higher selection
   probability. The cited pages name 8–15 specific lenders, mention
   the Bank of England base rate, name the Start Up Loan scheme, name
   FCA, etc. Entity-density wins.
3. **Three-or-more-source corroboration.** 88% of AIO citations come
   from queries with 3+ sources cited. AIO assembles answers across
   sources rather than mirroring one.
4. **Question-form H2s.** Pages cited tend to have H2s like "What is
   a business loan?", "How much can I borrow?", "Are business loans
   tax deductible?" — directly mirroring PAA. money.co.uk's page is
   the textbook example: 12 FAQs, all in question form.
5. **Low schema sophistication.** Most cited pages use `FAQPage` and
   little else. Few use `Review` or `AggregateRating` properly. This
   is BBL's structural advantage — proper schema stack will out-cite
   the templated comparators.

### Schema and structure to copy (and improve)

**Schema stack BBL should use on every review page:**
- `FAQPage` — 6–10 questions, mirroring real PAA verbatim (use Always
  Data / Ahrefs PAA pulls; don't paraphrase). Highest-citation schema
  type per 2026 data.
- `Review` (single review of a lender, by named author, with `datePublished`
  and `dateModified` always current).
- `AggregateRating` (BBL editorial rating + visible Trustpilot pull-in
  count; numbers must match visible UI exactly).
- `FinancialProduct` (per-lender — naming the loan product, APR range,
  min/max amount, term).
- `Organization` (BBL itself, with editorial team named, FCA broker
  permission noted if/when granted, parent / sister-site disclosure).
- `Person` (every author byline → `Person` schema with `sameAs` to
  LinkedIn and any prior bylines).
- `BreadcrumbList` (consistent siloing).

**Structure pattern to copy from money.co.uk + improve from
ExpertSure / NerdWallet:**

1. H1 with month + year stamp ("Best business loans UK — April 2026").
2. 60–80-word direct-answer summary (AIO hook).
3. Quick comparison table (8–12 lenders, 5 columns: APR / amount / term
   / fees / our rating).
4. "How we picked" methodology block (named author, criteria, dates
   tested) — **money.co.uk doesn't have this; it's a structural moat.**
5. Lender-by-lender mini-reviews (~150 words each, with internal link
   to the full single-lender review page).
6. Pillar context section: rate environment (Bank of England base rate
   3.75% as of Feb 2026, Start Up Loans rate 7.5% from 6 April 2026,
   Growth Guarantee Scheme context).
7. FAQs (6–10, lifted verbatim from PAA, with FAQPage schema).
8. "Last updated" and "Next review due" dates.
9. Internal-link block to (a) related listicles, (b) single-lender
   reviews, (c) FundBiz application page for the corresponding product.

### Extra moats AI Search rewards

- **Named author + Person schema.** NerdWallet UK does it inconsistently;
  money.co.uk and MSM mostly don't. ChatGPT and Perplexity weight
  author entity strongly.
- **Trustpilot pull-in.** Show a live Trustpilot widget per reviewed
  lender; the entity-graph signal is huge.
- **Methodology page.** A standalone `/methodology/` or `/how-we-test/`
  page (Bankrate-style) — strong E-E-A-T signal, gets cited by LLMs as
  the trust anchor for the brand.
- **Last-updated visibility.** AIO heavily weights `dateModified`. A
  visible "Last updated: 25 April 2026 · Next review due: July 2026"
  stamp at the top of every review beats undated competitors.

## 7. Site architecture (silo / pillar-cluster)

Four primary silos, each with one pillar page and a cluster of supporting
review / listicle / definition pages. Every cluster page links upwards to
its pillar; every pillar links sideways into FundBiz application forms.

```
/ (home — "Independent UK business loan reviews")
│
├── /reviews/                       ← Silo 1: LENDER REVIEWS (the AI-citation engine)
```

### Silo logic

- **Silo 1 (Reviews)** is the AI-citation engine. Every page = `Review`
  + `AggregateRating` + `FAQPage` + `FinancialProduct` schema, dated,
  named author, methodology link.
- **Silo 2 (Best-for listicles)** is the head-term + persona-axis play.
  The pillar `/best/business-loans-uk-2026/` updates monthly; cluster
  pages updated quarterly.
- **Silo 3 (Guides / explainers)** owns the "what is" and pillar surface.
  These are AIO citation-magnets; they internal-link to relevant
  reviews and listicles.
- **Methodology page** is the trust anchor — every review and listicle
  links to it, and LLMs reliably surface it as the brand-trust answer.

### Cross-site linking rule (BBL → FundBiz)

Every BBL review and listicle has a single, consistent CTA pattern:
> "Compare [N] lenders side-by-side and apply through our sister site
> FundBiz" — with one link to the matching FundBiz application surface
> (e.g. `/declined/`, `/loans/hospitality/`, `/eligibility-checker/`).

Editorial-independence guard rails: BBL never hosts a form; never says
"apply now" without the sister-site disclosure; rating decisions are
documented on the methodology page and not tied to commission tier.

## 9. Open questions

1. **Editorial-team naming.** AI Search rewards real, named authors with
   Person schema and traceable bylines. Do we have 1–2 named editorial
   leads (LinkedIn-verifiable, ideally with prior fintech / SMB
   journalism credits) ready to launch behind, or do we recruit?
2. **Trustpilot widget licensing.** Pulling live Trustpilot data into
   review pages strengthens the entity-graph signal massively but
   requires a paid Trustpilot Business plan or careful use of public
   APIs. Decide build-vs-buy.
3. **Cross-site editorial-independence story.** BBL must look genuinely
   independent of FundBiz to LLMs and Google. Question: separate
   `Organization` schemas with shared parent disclosure? Or fully
   separate brand-graphs with cross-links treated as standard external
   links? The wrong call leaks BBL's E-E-A-T into FundBiz's commercial
   layer.
4. **FCA broker permission — does BBL need it?** Strict editorial / no
   forms = arguably no permission needed for BBL itself. But CTA links
   into FundBiz that contain pre-filled application data may pull BBL
   into the broker chain. Confirm with FCA / counsel before launch.
5. **Sharia-compliant review depth.** A real Sharia-compliant review
   needs Murabaha / Ijara structural understanding, not just rate
   comparison. Source: external Islamic-finance scholar review for the
   first three Sharia review pages? Cost vs trust-signal trade-off.
6. **CDFI partnership commercials.** BCRS, GC Business Finance, SWIG,
   Let's Do Business — are any of these on Awin / Impact, or do we
   need direct partnership commercials? Most CDFIs are mission-driven
   not commercial, so commission may be modest or grant-funded.
7. **Methodology page rigour.** Bankrate-style methodology requires
   real testing (applications submitted, response times measured,
   total cost of credit calculated for matched profiles). How
   rigorously do we test? Defining "we tested 14 lenders" honestly
   is the brand promise — need a documented internal testing
   protocol before any review goes out.
8. **Update cadence and "last reviewed" infrastructure.** AIO weights
   `dateModified` heavily. Need a CMS workflow that visibly stamps
   pages with last-reviewed and next-review-due dates, and a quarterly
   refresh schedule for all 25 launch pages.
9. **Schema strategy depth.** Skipped this round per scope, but the
   `FAQPage` + `Review` + `AggregateRating` + `FinancialProduct` +
   `Person` + `Organization` stack needs a follow-up brief covering
   exact JSON-LD templates and validation harness.
10. **Niche-grid attack ordering.** Women-led, Sharia-compliant, ethnic
    minority — which to ship first? Sharia has the cleanest citation
    vacuum but smallest absolute volume; women-led has the biggest
    volume but is starting to get product-page competition. Probably
    ship women-led first as the wedge listicle, Sharia second as the
    citation flag-plant, ethnic-minority/CDFI third for the breadth
    play. Confirm.
11. **Trustpilot for BBL itself.** Does BBL need its own Trustpilot
    presence (the brand will get review-shaped citations), and how is
    that seeded if BBL doesn't transact? Maybe seed via FundBiz user
    surveys post-funding ("Did our BBL review match your experience?").
12. **Multilingual consideration.** Per the niche-brief Fleet rules,
    multilingual content does NOT belong on BBL (it would dilute the
    English-entity graph). But Sharia-compliant in Urdu / Arabic, and
    Polish-builder loan content, are real opportunities — confirm
    these route to Fleet microsites, not to BBL.
