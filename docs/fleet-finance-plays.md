# Fleet Finance Plays — BBL · FunBiz · Card Terminals · Asset Finance

**Date:** April 2026
**Branch:** `claude/niche-development-setup-PJpQh`

This doc covers **only** the niches, pages, and content plays developed for the existing finance-product fleet plus the new Asset Finance addition. Consumer-affiliate sites (Pet Hub, Wedding, Gym, etc.) and unrelated standalones are tracked in `docs/niche-shortlist-2026-04.md`.

The four fleets covered here:
- **BBL** — business loans (term, unsecured, MCA, working capital)
- **FunBiz** — invoice finance + broader funding products
- **Card Terminals** — merchant services

## 4. Asset Finance Hub (NEW BUILD)

The new fleet extension. **Per closed deal: £400 – £20,000 broker fee.** Tickets £20k–£500k+.

### 4.1 Why it slots in here

Asset finance is the natural next product for an SME that:
- Has a card terminal but needs to upgrade EPOS / fit-out (feed from §3)
- Has a business loan but is hitting personal-guarantee limits (feed from §1)
- Has IF but needs equipment, not working capital (feed from §2)
- Is buying a vehicle / plant / machinery / IT for the business

The broker process is similar to existing fleet operations. The lender panel overlaps significantly (Time Finance, Aldermore, Close Brothers, Praetura, MUFG).

## 6. The cross-sell stack

When a lead lands from any cross-fleet feeder, the in-house team qualifies once and routes to multiple fleet products. Sample patterns:

| Lead source | First product | Common cross-sells |
|---|---|---|
| Card terminals enquiry (existing site) | Card machine | MCA (BBL §1.10) · Asset finance for till / EPOS · Working capital · Business insurance |
| Business loan enquiry (existing site) | Term loan / MCA | Card terminals · Asset finance · IF (FunBiz) · Business insurance |
| Asset finance enquiry (new) | HP / lease | Top-up business loan · Vehicle tracking partner · Business insurance |
| Tech Directory enquiry | Custom build placement | BBL to fund the build · Asset finance for IT · Card terminals · AI-search-ready website |
| FindATradey enquiry | Lead-buy subscription | Asset finance for van + tools · Mobile card terminal · BBL working capital |
| Site 1 trade-side lead | Builder lead-buy | Same as FindATradey trade side |
| NEW 24 Gym operator lead | Gym launch advisory | Asset finance for equipment · Card terminals + EPOS · BBL fit-out |

One captured lead can produce 2–3 closed products. **The cross-sell stack is where the per-visitor LTV actually comes from** — not from any single fleet page.
